# Annabel Filippini

## Practical AI systems for expert-led small businesses

I help small business owners figure out where AI actually belongs, where it does not, and what first system would save time without making the business more complicated.

I work best with expert-led businesses: coaches, consultants, wellness practices, boutique service businesses, founder-led ecommerce brands, and local/community businesses where the owner's judgment, voice, or standards are a big part of why customers trust them.

## What I Do

**AI Starting Point Audit**  
I map your website, customer journey, repeated communication, internal docs, and owner bottlenecks to find the first practical place AI or better systems can help.

**Website + Customer Journey Cleanup**  
I improve the pages and flows customers already touch: homepage messaging, product/service pages, mobile experience, calls to action, Shopify structure, SEO basics, and owner-friendly editing.

**Practical AI Workflows**  
I build small workflows for the repetitive work that drains time: content drafts, outreach, follow-up, research, FAQs, internal support, and client communication prep.

## How I Work

- Diagnose before building.
- Start with the smallest useful system.
- Build inside tools the business already uses.
- Automate repetitive prep, not human judgment.
- Keep approval gates around sensitive or external communication.
- Leave behind something the owner or team can keep using.

## Good Fit
