# Annabel One-Pager Positioning Brief

Date: 2026-05-22

## Recommended Core Positioning

Annabel helps expert-led small businesses figure out where AI actually belongs, then turns messy workflows, websites, and repeated knowledge into practical systems people can use.

The one-pager should sell Annabel as a trusted diagnostic builder for businesses where the owner's judgment, voice, taste, service standards, or operational know-how are a major part of why customers trust the company.

## Strongest One-Line Version

I help expert-led small businesses turn messy workflows, websites, and repeated knowledge into practical AI-supported systems that save time without losing the human judgment that makes the business good.

## What The One-Pager Should Sell

The main offer should be an AI Operating System Starting Point, not generic AI consulting.

The simplest buyer-facing promise:

I map where AI and better systems can actually help your business, identify the highest-leverage first workflow, and either build the first useful version or introduce you to the right implementation partner.

## Recommended One-Page Structure

### 1. Header

Annabel Filippini

Practical AI systems, website cleanup, and workflow audits for expert-led small businesses.

### 2. Opening Positioning

I help expert-led small businesses figure out where AI belongs, what should stay human, and what first system would actually save time.

I usually start by auditing the business surface people already touch: the website, customer journey, repeated communications, internal docs, and the workflows the owner keeps having to explain.

### 3. Three Things I Help With

1. AIOS Starting Point Audit
   Map acquisition, delivery, support, and operations; identify the keystone workflow; produce a clear next-step roadmap.

2. Website + Customer Journey Cleanup
   Improve messaging, product/service pages, mobile flow, owner-editability, SEO basics, and conversion paths.

3. Practical AI Workflows
   Build content, outreach, follow-up, research, and internal support workflows with human approval where judgment matters.

### 4. Selected Work

Use four featured mini case studies:

- Cooldown: Shopify redesign, product clarity, run club/location structure, owner-friendly editing, QA.
- Marta Brummell: AI workspace for podcast, speaking, and sensitive session follow-up; voice and privacy guardrails.
- Charter Flight Support: operational voice, FAQ/sales education logic, AIOS opportunity map, and communication workflows for a high-trust private aviation support business.
- Website Audit Studio / Pepper Pong: audit-to-redesign workflow, AI discoverability, branded portal, reusable skills.

### 5. How I Work

- Diagnose before building.
- Build inside the tools the team already uses.
- Automate repetitive prep, not human judgment.
- Keep approval gates around external, sensitive, or high-trust work.
- Leave behind a system the owner/team can keep using.

### 6. Best Fit

Best fit:

- Expert-led small businesses where the owner's judgment, voice, taste, service standards, or operational know-how still shape the work.
- Coaches, consultants, wellness practices, boutique service businesses, founder-led ecommerce brands, local/community brands, and small teams with repeated knowledge work.
- Teams with repeated workflows, existing docs, customer communication, content needs, or a website that no longer matches the business.

### 7. CTA

Send me your website, workflow, or current AI question. I will tell you where I think AI belongs, where it does not, and where the first useful system should start.

## What To Avoid

- Do not lead with "AI agency" or "automation expert." It sounds generic.
- Do not over-index on being early-career. Mention Michigan and Okta as credibility, but lead with work.
- Do not list every project equally. The one-pager should sell trust and usefulness, not prove busyness.
- Do not use unsupported metrics. If a result is not measured, describe the concrete work done.
- Do not promise implementation outcomes from agencies unless scope, partner, price, and responsibility are confirmed.

## Best Tagline Options

1. Practical AI systems for businesses with real human judgment.
2. I help expert-led small businesses find the first AI system worth building.
3. Websites, workflows, and AI systems that make good work easier to repeat.
4. AI support for the repetitive work, more room for the work only people can do.
