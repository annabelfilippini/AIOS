# Consulting Outreach SOP

Last updated: 2026-04-27

## Purpose

Help Annabel find and contact potential consulting clients for her AI operating systems consulting company.

## Authorized Sender

Annie may send outreach from:

- `anniestarostin@gmail.com`

## Scope

Annie may send emails to potential clients when using:

- an approved target list
- approved positioning
- an approved email template or template pattern
- truthful project/client context from AI-OS

## Inputs

- `AI-OS/projects/consulting/`
- consulting offer and positioning docs
- prospect research
- approved target list
- approved outreach template
- prior replies or notes from Annabel

## Approved Outreach Boundaries

Annie may:

- research potential clients
- prepare short prospect notes
- send first-touch outreach emails from Annie's account
- send polite follow-ups based on an approved cadence
- send Annabel a summary of outreach sent, replies, and next actions

Annie must not:

- invent case studies, credentials, client results, pricing, or availability
- promise deliverables Annabel has not approved
- negotiate terms
- send contracts, invoices, or proposals without approval
- represent herself as Annabel
- imply Annabel personally wrote a message if Annie wrote it
- contact sensitive/regulated prospects with claims requiring professional review

## Default Email Style

- specific and brief
- grounded in a real observation about the prospect
- low-pressure
- no exaggerated AI hype
- no fake familiarity
- no overclaiming

## First-Touch Template

```text
Subject: quick idea for [business name]

Hi [Name],

I'm Annie, Annabel Filippini's assistant. Annabel has been looking closely at how expert-led businesses can turn their existing SOPs, onboarding docs, and repeated workflows into practical AI support for their team.

I noticed [specific observation about their business/workflow].

One small workflow that might be useful is [specific workflow idea]. The goal would be to help your team [practical benefit] while keeping human review in place for anything sensitive.

Would you be open to a short intro? Annabel is currently doing a few pilot-style audits to find the highest-leverage workflow and build one useful first version.

Best,
Annie
Assistant to Annabel Filippini
```

## Follow-Up Cadence

Default:

- first follow-up after 4-7 business days
- one final polite close-the-loop email after another 7-10 business days
- no more than two follow-ups unless Annabel approves

## Reporting

After outreach, Annie should update:

- `agents/annie/workspace/reports/`
- relevant prospect folder under `AI-OS/projects/consulting/prospects/` when one exists

Report format:

```text
Date:
Prospects contacted:
Messages sent:
Replies received:
Follow-ups due:
Needs Annabel:
```

## Approval

No approval required for first-touch or follow-up emails that follow this SOP, use approved facts, and do not include pricing, contracts, proposals, sensitive claims, or commitments.

Approval required for:

- proposals
- pricing
- client commitments
- case-study claims
- contracts
- invoices
- anything that changes Annabel's calendar directly
