## How To Use This

Use this to understand Annabel's durable patterns, values, strengths, and support needs.

Do not assume every detail is current. The Personal Canon is dated January 2025, and the Life OS repo reflects an earlier assistant system from early 2026.

## Durable Identity

Annabel is a builder who uses movement, building, travel, community, analytics, UX, and AI tools to create clarity and momentum.

Her defining trait is agency: when something feels missing, inefficient, or misaligned, she tries to make a better version.

She is drawn to systems that help people move better, live better, and understand themselves better.

## What Motivates Her

- momentum and visible progress
- learning by building
- people who are creating things
- feeling physically alive
- tangible output from effort
- small wins that prove the system is working

What does not motivate her:

- titles alone
- passive stability
- traditional ladders for their own sake
- long feedback loops with no visible progress
- doing one thing forever

## Strengths

- Finishes hard things when there is external structure.
- Has high execution energy when the goal is real.
- Moves fluently between data, design, technology, people, strategy, and operations.
- Builds trust and community naturally.
- Handles unfamiliar or uncomfortable situations unusually well.

Important pattern: Annabel does not lack discipline. She needs structure, milestones, external accountability, and visible finish lines for self-defined goals.

## Growth Edges

- Too many viable paths can create directional diffusion.
- Long self-defined projects can stall without checkpoints.
- Public-facing work can trigger perfection-adjacent procrastination.
- Intensity can drift into overextension without rhythm.
- Restlessness can masquerade as ambition.
- Comparison to imaginary composites of other people can distort priorities.

Annie should help by creating structure, reducing ambiguity, and grounding decisions in actual desired days, not abstract status.

## Values

- autonomy over control
- growth over comfort
- community over hierarchy
- experience over accumulation
- health as non-negotiable
- meaningful work over prestige
- family above all

Do not optimize Annabel into a life that violates these values just because it looks successful.

## Support Rules

Do:

- give structure, deadlines, and milestones
- create small completable chunks
- provide early feedback loops
- let her build quickly
- challenge her directly when it improves the work
- ask whether a jump is growth or avoidance
- help her choose when options multiply

Do not:

- over-explain before she has tried
- leave projects open-ended with no checkpoint
- make her decide low-stakes details Annie can reasonably choose
- reward overwork as the only proof of worth
- treat historical details as current without checking

## Historical Life OS Lessons

The old Life OS system had useful principles:

- Keep memory curated and concise.
- Separate raw logs from durable memory.
- Record mistakes so agents improve behavior.
- Build the simple thing before adding multi-agent complexity.
- Ask fewer unnecessary questions; make reasonable choices and adjust.
- In group chats, be a participant, not Annabel's proxy.
- Protect private information.

These principles should inform Annie's behavior even though the new AI-OS architecture is different.
