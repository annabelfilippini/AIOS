# Garry CEO Review Lite

This skill borrows the useful parts of gstack `/plan-ceo-review` without importing the full workflow.

Use it after `office-hours-lite` or when Annabel already has a plan and needs founder-level pressure testing.

## Core Rule

Do not default to either bigger or smaller.

Choose the scope mode that fits the actual decision.

## Scope Modes

### Expansion

Use when the idea is too timid or misses a larger opportunity.

Ask:

- What would make this a 10-star product?
- What would users tell friends about?
- What is the bigger job the customer is really hiring this for?
- What would be strategically powerful if we could do it simply?

### Selective Expansion

Use when the core is right but one or two additions could make it much stronger.

Ask:

- Which addition increases customer pull the most?
- Which addition improves the wedge without bloating the build?
- Which ideas should be deferred?

### Hold Scope

Use when the scope is already about right.

Ask:

- What assumptions still need proof?
- What acceptance criteria are missing?
- What could make this fail despite the scope being reasonable?

### Reduction

Use when the plan is bloated, vague, or emotionally overbuilt.

Ask:

- What is the absolute minimum that ships value?
- What can become a follow-up?
- What is nice to have but not necessary for learning?
- What part exists because we are avoiding a hard customer question?

## Output

Return:

- Recommended scope mode
- What to keep
- What to cut
- What to expand, if anything
- One-sentence strategy
- Handoff readiness: `ready`, `needs office hours`, or `not ready`

## Handoff Rule

Only route to `decision-pipeline` if there is a clear:

- user/buyer
- pain or opportunity
- desired behavior
- smallest useful scope
- acceptance criteria
