# Business Partner Workspace

Active work products the Business Partner is preparing.

Use subfolders for:

- `reviews/` - working notes for Business Partner plan reviews
- `implementation-notes/` - implementation note drafts or mirrors
- `decision-memos/` - CEO decision memos, tradeoff notes, and critique artifacts

The durable review and implementation notes for Garry handoffs should usually live next to the handoff in `AI-OS/agents/shared/handoffs/`.
