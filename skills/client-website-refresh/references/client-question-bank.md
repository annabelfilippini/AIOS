# Client Question Bank

Use plain business questions. Avoid schema-shaped interviews.

## Website And Brand

- What do you dislike most about the current site?
- What parts should absolutely stay?
- What feels inaccurate, outdated, or embarrassing?
- What do customers ask for that the current site does not answer?
- Who needs to be able to edit the site after launch?
- Which pages, photos, testimonials, bios, services, or offers are missing?
- Are any claims, services, prices, or policies sensitive enough to need sign-off?
- Which claims, testimonials, outcomes, before/after stories, or condition lists
  are approved for public use?

## Workflow And Backend

- What tools does the website point to today: booking, portal, checkout, CRM,
  inventory, forms, email, text, payments?
- Which of those tools are working well enough to keep?
- Where does a customer or patient go after clicking the main CTA?
- Who on the team owns updates, approvals, billing, fulfillment, and support?
- Where do staff add recommendations, orders, products, discounts, or notes?
- Which parts should Annabel set up, and which parts belong to the vendor or
  client's internal admin?
- If the site is health, finance, legal, or otherwise regulated, who signs off
  on claims before launch?

## Store, Products, Or Patient/Client Portals

Adapted from Vital Health:

- Are products or services bought publicly, inside a logged-in portal, or both?
- When a provider or staff member recommends a product, where is that recorded?
- Who fulfills the order: the office, a third-party vendor, drop-shipping, or a
  mix?
- When promotions run, are they public announcements, checkout discounts,
  patient-specific discounts, backend coupons, or all of the above?
- Who needs backend/admin access for product updates and promotions?

## Proposal And Pricing

- What does the client need priced now versus after discovery?
- What is the spending ceiling or approval threshold?
- Who is the payer or final approver?
- What should be fixed-fee because the shape is known?
- What should be hourly because the workflow depends on discovery?
- What client assets or approvals could block launch?
