# Good And Bad Examples

## Good

- Rebuilt Vital Health as native Webflow pages rather than treating the Vercel
  mockup as the final site.
- Kept Webflow as public site and Cerbo as patient portal.
- Made provider bios, services, contact details, body copy, stats, and images
  editable.
- Used a small script for scroll reveal and mobile nav after native editability
  was preserved for content.
- Published to staging only while testimonials and medical-claim sign-off were
  still blockers.
- Rechecked desktop, mobile, links, SEO metadata, console errors, and copy
  punctuation before sharing the staging link.

## Bad

- Assuming Webflow CLI behaves like Shopify theme push.
- Building everything as injected custom code just because Designer is flaky.
- Sharing a staging site without saying which placeholders remain.
- Letting old SEO titles keep an em dash while body copy has been cleaned.
- Calling full-page screenshots blank when scroll animation simply delays
  rendering. Scroll the page or take viewport screenshots after reveal.
- Publishing a custom domain with placeholder testimonials or sensitive medical
  stats that have not been signed off.
