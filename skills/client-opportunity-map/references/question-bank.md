# Question Bank

Use these as prompts. Do not ask all of them.

## Business Shape

- What do you do, and how do you make money?
- Who pays you?
- What has to happen before you get paid?
- What parts of the business feel most repetitive?

## Tools

- What tools do you open every day?
- What tools do you open every Monday or Friday?
- Where do leads, customers, orders, appointments, tickets, or documents start?
- What tool do you pay for but rarely check?
- What tool has useful data that nobody looks at?

## Repeated Work

- What do you copy from one system into another?
- What report, email, or document do you write repeatedly?
- What do people ask you for over and over?
- What work piles up when you are busy?
- What work requires your approval before it goes out?

## Existing Automation

- Do you already have any Zapier, Make, n8n, scheduled jobs, scripts, or app
  automations?
- Which automations work well enough to keep?
- Which automations create cleanup work?

## Decision And Approval

- What should never be sent automatically?
- What must a human approve?
- What data is sensitive?
- What would be risky to automate too early?

## Best First Win

- If this system saved you two hours next week, where would those hours come
  from?
- What would make you trust the system?
- What would be useful even if nothing got fully automated yet?
