# Session: Copenhagen short trip plan

## What we worked on

Annabel is going to Copenhagen tonight, Friday June 5, 2026, staying at Urban
Camper Hostel near Norrebro Station, and leaving Sunday June 7 around noon. She
wanted a short itinerary based on saved TikToks, with a mix of food, affordable
shopping, baths/saunas, and key monuments/buildings.

TikTok access was limited, but short links resolved and oEmbed exposed useful captions and place lists.

## Decisions made

- Treat the trip as one full day plus two travel edges, not 2.5 full days.
- Tonight: keep it easy after arrival: metro into the city, then Nyhavn and
  central waterfront / Amalienborg / Marble Church exterior if energy allows.
- Saturday is the main day: Rosenborg Slot + King's Garden, La Cabra or Studio
  x Kitchen, Torvehallerne, affordable shopping, Nyhavn / Amalienborg / Marble
  Church, then one bath/sauna or dinner.
- Sunday morning: keep it close and gentle before airport: Superkilen and/or
  Assistens Cemetery / Jaegersborggade, or a morning bath if not done Saturday.
- Best transit approach: City Pass Small plus walking; use Norrebro Station to
  Nørreport as the shortcut into the center.
- Do not prioritize Little Mermaid unless Annabel specifically wants the icon;
  it is skippable for this short timeline.
- Best bath recommendation for reliability: AIRE Ancient Baths. La Banchina or
  CopenHot are more Copenhagen-waterfront coded but less convenient and likely
  crowded because Distortion festival is June 3-7.
