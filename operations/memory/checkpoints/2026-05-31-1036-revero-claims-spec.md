# Session: Revero Claims Matrix And Homepage Spec

## What we did

Created:

- `projects/revero-website-refresh/audit/claims-matrix.md`
- `projects/revero-website-refresh/strategy/homepage-redesign-spec.md`

## Decisions

- Keep the first HTML preview conservative.
- Do not use "reverse disease," "heal," "get off meds," or patient outcome
  numbers in the hero.
- Treat onboarding, app, support, payment, labs, devices, and clinical workflow
  as external systems.
- Recommended positioning: a virtual clinic for chronic conditions built around
  medical support and personalized nutrition.
- Recommended primary CTA label: Get started.
- Recommended secondary CTA label: Book a free info call.

## Open blockers

- Confirm approved condition list and availability.
- Confirm testimonial permissions and disclaimers.
- Confirm pricing, device costs, lab-state limitations, HSA/FSA, and refund
  language.
- Decide whether a final rebuild would stay in the current funnel platform or
  move to Webflow/CMS.

Build a homepage HTML preview only, not a full site.
