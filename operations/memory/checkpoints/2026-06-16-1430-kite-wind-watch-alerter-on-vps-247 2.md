# Session: wind alerter now 24/7 on the VPS

## Why
Alerts deliver to Telegram (reaches her phone anywhere), but were GENERATED by a
Mac launchd job → only ran when the Mac was awake/online. Closed laptop = no
alerts. Moved generation to the always-on Hetzner VPS.

## What shipped
- `scraper/wind_alert.py` made portable: loads `TELEGRAM_BOT_TOKEN` /
  `SYNOPTIC_TOKEN` from a `.env` next to the script (or env vars) when the Mac
  config files are absent; `send_macos_notification` no-ops on non-darwin.
- **Fixed a TZ bug** (surfaced by the VPS being UTC): `now` now computed in
  America/Denver via `zoneinfo`, matching the naive Denver forecast timestamps.
  Was previously fine on the Mac (also Denver) but 6h off on a UTC server.
- Also note: an earlier IndexError in the state-prune (`_last_run` key had no
  "|") was already fixed in the blended rewrite — that had been crashing the
  launchd job on repeat runs.
- Deployed to VPS `root@5.78.218.220:/root/kite-wind-watch/`:
  `wind_alert.py` + `.env` (tokens, chmod 600, written over SSH, never printed).
  Hourly cron `5 * * * *`. Verified: VPS dry-run + REAL Telegram send ok=True.
- **Disabled the Mac launchd wind-alert job** (plist parked as `.disabled`) to
  avoid double pings. Daily Reddit `com.kitewindwatch.scrape` stays on the Mac
  (needs her browser cookie).

## Operate
- Update alerter: edit `scraper/wind_alert.py`, then
  `scp scraper/wind_alert.py root@5.78.218.220:/root/kite-wind-watch/`.
- VPS logs: `ssh root@5.78.218.220 'tail /root/kite-wind-watch/wind_alert.log'`.
- VPS state: `/root/.local/share/kite-wind-watch/alert_state.json`.
- Tokens on VPS: `/root/kite-wind-watch/.env`. Synoptic token regen:
  `/v2/auth?apikey=KEY`.

- Dashboard viewing is still local (localhost:8804); only alerting is on the VPS.
