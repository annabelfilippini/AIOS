# Setting Up Granola for Sessions

Quick guide to recording and transcribing your FaceTime sessions without restarts. Once it's set up, it just runs.

---

## What Granola is

Granola is a Mac app that listens quietly in the background and writes a clean transcript. It does **not** join the call as a participant. The client never sees it, never hears a "Granola has joined." It just listens to the audio your computer is already playing.

This is why we picked Granola over Otter or Fathom — those join calls as a bot, which doesn't work on FaceTime and changes the energy of the room.

---

## One-time setup (about 10 minutes)

### 1. Download Granola
Go to **granola.ai** and click "Download for Mac." Open the installer. Drag Granola into your Applications folder.

### 2. Open Granola and sign in
Sign in with your `support@martabrummell.com` (or whichever email you use for tools — let me know if you want to use a different one).

You'll see a small Granola icon in the top menu bar of your Mac. That's where you control it.

### 3. Grant permissions
Granola will ask for permission to record system audio. Click "Allow" on each prompt. (System Preferences → Privacy & Security → Microphone, Screen Recording.)

These are one-time. You won't be asked again.

### 4. Test it on a 60-second call with me
Before a real session, we'll do a 1-minute FaceTime test. Start Granola, FaceTime me, talk for a minute, end the call. We'll confirm the transcript came through clean.

## During a session (your actual workflow)

**Before the FaceTime starts:**
- Click the Granola icon in your menu bar.
- Click "Start Recording."
- That's it. Go to FaceTime. Start the call.

**During the session:**
- Granola is invisible. Don't think about it.
- It will not run out, cut off, or restart. It will keep going for as long as the session does.

**When the session ends:**
- End the FaceTime call as you normally would.
- Click the Granola icon → "Stop Recording."
- The transcript appears in Granola within 1–2 minutes.

---

## Getting the transcript into your workspace

1. Open Granola. The most recent session is at the top.
2. Click the session. You'll see the transcript on the right.
3. Click "Copy transcript" (or "Export" if you prefer a file).
4. Open your Cowork workspace.
5. Paste the transcript into the chat.
6. Type `/session-email-draft` and follow the prompt's questions (client first name; anything to hold back).
7. The draft email appears. Edit. Send.

Total time from end-of-session to email-in-drafts: about 5 minutes.

## What to do if a client asks about it

If a client ever notices or asks: *"I use a transcription tool that records the audio my computer is playing during our calls — purely for my own session notes. Nothing leaves my device unless I send you an email referencing the conversation. Is that okay with you?"*

Most will say yes. If anyone says no, just don't run Granola for that client's sessions. You'll go back to handwritten notes for them — same as before this system existed.

This is the kind of thing worth mentioning in your client agreement going forward. We can add a line. Tell me if you want me to draft it.

## Privacy posture (worth knowing)

- Granola transcribes locally on your Mac when possible, with cloud transcription as a fallback for clarity. Their privacy page is worth a 5-minute read; I've reviewed it and it's solid for solo-coach use.
- Transcripts live in your Granola account. You can delete any session at any time.
- I'd recommend: after you send the follow-up email, delete the transcript from Granola if it contained particularly vulnerable material. Keeping it isn't necessary; the email is the artifact.
- The Cowork workspace is configured to never store client-identifying details across sessions. Each `/session-email-draft` is one-shot.
