## The 30-minute audience clarity call (worksheet for Annabel to run)

Run this on the Week 1 setup call. Goal: leave with this file filled in to v1.0.

### Question set

1. **For each in-scope offer — name the one specific person.**
   "Picture one specific person who would be perfect for 1:1 / your podcast / a parent talk you'd give. Who are they? What's their week look like? What do they already believe about this work?"
   *Look for: she says "everyone" or "anyone going through hard things." Push back. Re-ask until specific.*

2. **The "who this is NOT for" test.**
   "Who would hate this? Who would bounce off it in 30 seconds?"
   *Defining the negative space is often easier than defining the positive.*

3. **The "moment of recognition" question.**
   "Tell me about the last time someone said something like 'this is for me' or 'I needed this today.' What did they say specifically? What were they responding to?"
   *Real testimonials > imagined audience.*

4. **The "what they almost said yes to instead" question.**
   "If someone joins The Practice, what were they about to do instead — therapy, another coach, a course, an app, just keep going?"
   *Adjacent options reveal the actual decision they're making.*

5. **The podcast audience reality check.**
   "Stated: anyone trying to be more present. But who *actually* writes in? Who shows up at speaking events and says 'I love your podcast'?"
   *The reality and the aspiration often diverge — that gap is the audience clarity work.*

6. **The visceral-experience aspiration (speaking).**
   "When you say you want talks to be visceral — what does that look like in the room? What's the moment you want?"
   *This grounds 'visceral' from concept to specific experience.*

7. **The 30-day audience question.**
   "If we narrow your audience by 30% but they're 3x more activated, would you take that trade?"
   *Tests scarcity posture vs growth posture. Should be a yes — confirms playbook framing.*
