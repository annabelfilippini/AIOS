# Marta's Offers

This file is a context anchor for Cowork. When Marta asks for content critique, audience clarity, or speaking prep, refer to this to ground "who this is for" and "what this is selling."

## The four things Marta does

### 1. The Practice (membership)
- **What:** $49/mo, application-only, hosted on Circle.
- **What's inside:** monthly themes, weekly prompts, daily reflections, group calls, content library, a member-only newsletter (drafted by Grace, not Marta).
- **Posture:** intentional, small, application-gated. **Scarcity is the product, not a constraint.**
- **Audience:** people who have done some work on themselves and want a contained, ongoing practice — not crisis-mode beginners.
- **Cowork's lane:** none. Grace handles newsletter and Circle content.

### 2. 1:1 Coaching
- **What:** $275 / 60-min on-demand. Monthly package available (weekly FaceTime + text/audio support; price on request).
- **Posture:** financially committed clients, with rare exceptions for the deeply committed.
- **Audience:** narrower than The Practice — people working through specific personal terrain who want a private coach.

### 3. The Practice of Being Human (podcast)
- **What:** 191 episodes, biweekly cadence officially (weekly in practice), since 2020. Solo-hosted. 5.0★ on Apple.
- **Audience:** somewhat undefined — parents, young adults, general listeners all show up. **This is one of the things Marta is actively trying to clarify.**
- **Production:** Grace edits.
- **Purpose:** the front porch. People meet Marta here. It is not directly monetized; it feeds The Practice and 1:1 indirectly.

### 4. Speaking
- **What:** parent talks, school events, podcast guesting. Increasing in volume.
- **Recent:** parent talk earned a return invite; upcoming 8th grade graduation speech.
- **Goal:** create *visceral experiences*, not concept teaching.
- **Posture:** wants to get sharper at this. Audience engagement is the growth edge.

## What Marta does NOT do

- Not a therapist anymore (was MSW; now coaches). Coaching is not therapy. Don't conflate.
- Not a course creator with cohorts and launches.
- Not a high-volume creator. No daily content. No hustle posture.
- Not chasing scale. The Practice is intentionally application-only.
- Not running a CRM, sales funnel, or pipeline. Relationships are the product.

## How to use this when critiquing

- When a piece of content drifts toward "general coaching audience," ask: which of the four offers does this serve? If the answer is "all of them," it probably serves none of them.
- Speaking content should bend toward visceral experience over concept. If a talk outline is concept-heavy, that's the thing to flag.
- 1:1 content (session emails) is not marketing — it's care. Different voice rules apply (see `session-email-draft.md`).
- **Newsletter is out of scope.** Grace drafts it. If Marta pastes a newsletter draft, redirect her to one of the in-scope skills.
