# How This Works

A one-page reference for your Practice workspace. Pin this to your inbox or save to your desktop.

---

## What you have

A private workspace inside **Claude Cowork** that knows how you sound on the podcast, in sessions, and on stage. Inside it, three named skills you call by typing a slash:

- `/podcast-pressure-test` — before recording an episode
- `/speaking-prep` — before a talk
- `/session-email-draft` — after a 1:1, to draft a follow-up email

Plus a recording tool — **Granola** — that runs quietly in the background while you're on FaceTime, so you never have to restart a recorder mid-session again.

**Not in here:** newsletter help. Grace handles that and we're leaving it alone.

---

## The shape of a normal week

**Monday (content + admin day)**
- Open the workspace. Type `/podcast-pressure-test` for any episode you're recording this week.
- If you have a talk coming up, type `/speaking-prep`.
- 30 seconds — no morning briefing, no notification, no thing to keep up with.

**Tuesday / Wednesday / Thursday (client days)**
- Granola runs in the background while you're on FaceTime.
- After each session: drop the Granola transcript into the workspace, type `/session-email-draft`, edit, send.
- That's it.

**Before a talk**
- Drop your outline in. Type `/speaking-prep`.
- Use the visceral-hook section. Use the rewritten opening and closing. Or don't. You decide.

## What it never does

- It never publishes anything.
- It never sends an email for you.
- It never changes your work without you reading it first.
- It never tells you what to do — it shows you what's there.

## What I need from you (one-time, ever)

- 3–5 of your favorite past podcast episodes — just send me the links. I'll listen and pull what makes you sound like *you*. This is what keeps the workspace from sounding like generic coaching content.
- 30 minutes on a Zoom for the audience clarity conversation. (One time, not weekly.)
- 2–3 sample session follow-up emails you've sent in the past (with client names and identifying details removed). These calibrate `/session-email-draft` to your real voice on the page.

That's the entire homework, ever. After that you're using it.

## When something feels off

If a draft sounds wrong — too soft, too generic, too much like coaching cliché — tell it. In the workspace, just type:

> *"That isn't my voice. The line about [X] is the kind of thing I'd never say. Try again, sharper."*

It will revise. The more specific your pushback, the better the next pass.

If three passes don't get there, send me a note. That's voice tuning — my job, not yours.
