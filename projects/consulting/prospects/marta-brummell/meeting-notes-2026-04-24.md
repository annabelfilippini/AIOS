- Records sessions, summarizes with AI, then edits for personal touch.

  - Podcast editing
  - Newsletter creation
  - Social media reels
- Grace takes content direction and is supportive, but does not provide critical feedback on quality, structure, or audience resonance.

## Emerging Implementation Areas

### 1. Recording + Session Summary Workflow

Fix the current recording cutoff issue and improve the transcript-to-summary-to-email workflow. This is immediate operational relief and builds on behavior she already trusts.

### 2. Content + Speaking Thought Partner

Create an AI-assisted review workflow for podcast ideas, newsletter drafts, talk outlines, transcripts, and speaking prep. The system should help Marta identify:

- Who the piece is really for
- What is emotionally landing
- What feels too abstract
- Where the story or argument loses tension
- How to make a talk more visceral and experiential
- Stronger openings, closings, examples, and audience questions
- What feedback Grace should provide before production

### 3. Newsletter Expansion Strategy

Help decide what should remain member-only versus what can become public-facing. If the newsletter expands beyond subscribers, preserve membership value while creating a clearer acquisition path from podcast/speaking into The Practice.

## Updated Diagnosis

Marta is not AI-new. She already uses ChatGPT in a meaningful, high-trust part of her work. The opportunity is not basic AI adoption; it is moving from ad hoc task-level use to a more reliable operating system around client follow-up, content quality, speaking prep, and audience clarity.

The best first wedge may be **content and speaking leverage**, not pure podcast repurposing. The bottleneck is less "who edits the content?" and more "who helps make the content sharper before Grace produces it?"

## Potential Follow-Up Framing

> You are already using AI thoughtfully: not to replace your voice, but to help you be more present with clients and faster with follow-up. My sense is the next step is not adding a bunch of new tools. It is building a more reliable system around the places where your work is expanding: client follow-up, content, and speaking.
