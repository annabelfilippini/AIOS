## What I heard

You are already using AI thoughtfully — not to replace your voice, but to be more present with clients and faster with follow-up. The next step is not a new tool stack. It is a more reliable system around the places your work is actually expanding: **podcast, speaking, and 1:1 follow-up.**

Grace handles production beautifully — including newsletter drafting end-to-end. What's missing is a critical thought partner *before* a podcast episode is recorded or a talk is delivered — someone who reads an outline or a transcript and tells you where it lands, where it drifts, and where the visceral hook should be. Plus a tighter loop on the session→email workflow you already have working.

That is what this is.

---

## The Outcome

In 30 days, you will:

- Walk into every speaking engagement with a structurally tested talk — clear audience, clear emotional arc, sharper opening and close.
- Pressure-test every podcast idea before recording, so episodes you publish are the ones you most want out in the world.
- Have a sharper post-recording handoff to Grace — what landed, what to cut, what the pull-quotes are.
- Stop restarting the recorder mid-session, and have a session follow-up email drafted within minutes of every 1:1.

Not measured in hours saved. Measured in **podcast and speaking that land harder, and clients who feel met after every session.**

## The Wedge

**One thing: a private workspace with a small library of named skills you can call in the chatbox.**

We'll set you up on **Claude Cowork** — a workspace built for exactly this kind of work. It's a step up in critique sharpness from what you're using now, with one place that knows your voice, your podcast, your audiences, and your rules. You call skills directly in the chatbox the way you'd call a teammate.

The workspace knows:
- How you sound on the podcast and in session emails
- Who your podcast and speaking audiences actually are (and who they're not)
- Your podcast's strengths, recurring patterns, and the frames you lean on
- The questions Grace doesn't ask but should

The skills you'll use:

1. **`/speaking-prep`** — paste a talk outline; get visceral hook, audience clarity, structural critique, stronger opening/closing, audience questions.
2. **`/podcast-pressure-test`** — paste an episode idea *before* recording; get who-is-this-for, what could go wrong, the strongest version of the argument.
3. **`/episode-postmortem`** — paste the transcript after recording; get what landed, what to cut, what to tell Grace before she edits.
4. **`/session-email-draft`** — drop a session transcript in; get a follow-up email in your voice, ready to edit and send.

You decide what changes. The workspace never publishes anything. **Newsletter drafting stays with Grace — that's not what this is for.** This is your private editor for the work you do directly: speaking, podcast, and client follow-up.

## The session recording fix

Your current recorder cutting off every few minutes is the friction. Here's the new flow:

1. **Granola** runs quietly in the background while you're on FaceTime. No bot in the call. No "Otter has joined the meeting." Just system audio, transcribed cleanly, no cutoff.
2. When the session ends, you drag the Granola transcript into Cowork.
3. You type `/session-email-draft`.
4. A draft email — in your voice, built around the moments that mattered, not a generic recap — is waiting for you.
5. You edit and send.

Same shape as what you're doing now. Fewer steps. No restarts. Sharper draft because the workspace knows how you write.

## The 30-Day Plan

### Week 1 — Setup
- I build the Cowork workspace: voice, audience, offers, rules, and the four skills.
- I do a listen pass on 3–5 of your recent episodes to anchor the workspace in how you actually sound.
- I get Granola installed and tested with one real FaceTime session.
- We do a 20-minute walkthrough on Zoom.

### Weeks 2–4 — Real work
- You use the skills on real upcoming work: an episode you're about to record, a talk you're prepping, transcripts after you record.
- You run `/session-email-draft` after every 1:1.
- We do one 30-minute voice-tuning call so the workspace sounds more like you each pass.
- Adjust skills based on what's actually useful and what isn't.

### Day 30 — Review
- Look at: did it sharpen episodes before recording? Did it change what you sent Grace after? How fast are session emails landing now? Did one talk feel different?
- Decide: keep going as-is, extend to other parts of your work, or wind down.
