## Working definition

Cooldown-like means a founder-led or community-led running/wellness business with some mix of:

- IRL community, run clubs, ambassadors, chapters, or events
- ecommerce/apparel/merch or strong sponsor/brand partnership motion
- small enough that Shopify, content ops, sponsor outreach, launch systems, or AI-assisted workflows could matter
- values-led positioning rather than generic performance-only running

> I helped Cooldown turn a messy Shopify/community growth problem into concrete fixes: product page cleanup, launch-ready content, sponsor outreach workflows, and AI systems for repetitive ops. I’m looking for 2-3 similar community-led running or wellness brands where I can do the same kind of practical cleanup and automation.

Best wedge offers:

1. Free mini audit: 5 Shopify/content/community ops fixes with one small prototype.
2. Sponsor outreach system: research + draft + follow-up tracker for chapter launches.
3. Launch package system: product drop/chapter/event content kit from one input.
4. Ambassador/community ops: lightweight intake, tagging, reply templates, and weekly digest.
