---

## Goal of the call

Walk them through the Cowork workspace I already built → agree on which skill I expand next → understand if there's paid work after.

**Not the goal:** quote a price, close a contract, dump the audit.

**Anchor question if I get lost:** *"What's the one thing in your week you wish you could stop doing?"*

> "The audit was the front door, but I actually went and built something. I want to show it to you, walk through how Bailey and Anya would each use it, and then talk about what we'd add next if it's useful."

> "Pick one to add next. Not three. The three that are already there cover the biggest hours and the biggest dollars — but you two know your week better than I do. Which one would actually free up the most time?"

Then:
- Confirm which skill I'm adding
- Ask what I need from them: brand voice docs, sample emails/captions, anything missing for `audience.md` and `offers.md`
- Confirm I'll send same-day follow-up with recap + delivery date (5–7 days out)
- Free for this one. Price comes after evidence.

## Watch for

- **Bailey wants to talk site, not workflow** → "happy to fix the audit punch list, but the 10x is the back office"
- **Anya quiet** → ask her directly: "what would you most want off your plate?"
- **Budget question** → "let me build the next one free first, we price after evidence"
- **"We've tried AI tools that didn't work"** → goldmine. Ask which ones and why
- **They list 10 things they want** → "Love it. Pick one."
