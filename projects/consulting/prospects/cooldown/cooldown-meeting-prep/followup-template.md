## Notes on writing this email

- **Be specific.** Generic recap = generic response. Quote them back to themselves.
- **One thing, not three.** You promised in the meeting to build *one* thing. Don't scope-creep in the email.
- **Concrete delivery date.** Not "next week" — a day.
- **The "what I need from you" list is the test.** If they send it = real intent. If they don't within 3 days, nudge once, then deprioritize.
- **Don't mention money until it works.** You anchored in the meeting that fair pricing comes after evidence. Stay there.
- **Keep the email short.** Under 300 words for the body (not counting scope). This is a recap, not a proposal.
