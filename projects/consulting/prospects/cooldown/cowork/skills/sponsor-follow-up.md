# /sponsor-follow-up

**When to use:** After a first sponsor email has been sent, when Bailey needs a follow-up, a reply drafted, or the sponsor tracker updated with next steps.

**Owner:** Bailey

**How to use:** Paste the previous email, any sponsor reply, and the tracker row if available. This skill drafts the next move and outputs a clean pipeline update. It never sends.

---

## Prompt to paste

```
/sponsor-follow-up

Sponsor:
[brand name]

Mode:
- No reply
- Positive reply
- Maybe / needs more info
- Not right now
- Intro to someone else
- Need tracker cleanup only

Previous email:
[paste the sent email or summary]

Sponsor reply:
[paste reply, or "no reply"]

Original ask:
[product seeding, chapter launch, table, giveaway, collab, 15-min call, etc.]

Context:
[dates, launch details, prior relationship, deadline, city/campus, what Bailey wants to happen next]

Draft the next step. Use this exact structure:

1. READ OF THE SITUATION
   2-3 bullets. What happened, what matters, and what the best next ask should be.

2. RECOMMENDED NEXT MOVE
   One sentence. Be decisive: follow up, wait, ask for intro, send details, close loop, or move to later.

3. EMAIL DRAFT
   Subject ≤50 chars. Body ≤110 words. Sentence case. Warm-professional Cool Down voice. One clear ask. Sign as Bailey.

4. TRACKER UPDATE
   Return one row in this format:

   Sponsor | Contact | Mode | Last touch | Status | Next action | Follow-up date | Ask | Anchor | Notes

5. WHAT TO CONFIRM
   Any missing contact, date, city, sponsor deliverable, address for product shipment, or person responsible.

CRITICAL:
- Never guilt a sponsor for not replying.
- Do not over-explain cooldown again if they already received the first email.
- For no-reply follow-ups, add a fresh angle or concrete detail; don't just reword the first email.
- If they said no, respect it and leave the door open without pushing.
- Never auto-send anything.
```

---

## Notes

- Suggested follow-up rhythm for first-touch sponsor emails:
  - First follow-up: 4-6 business days after send.
  - Second follow-up: 7-10 business days later, only if the sponsor is high-fit or time-sensitive.
  - After that: move to "later" unless there is a new launch, city, or event angle.
- For launch-related sponsors, follow-up dates should work backward from the launch date, not from a generic cadence.
- For positive replies, the next email should usually ask for one concrete commitment: call time, product quantity, shipping address, rep availability, or intro to the right person.

## What this saves

Sponsor outreach gets messy when follow-ups live in Bailey's head or Gmail search. This keeps every thread moving with one next action, one follow-up date, and one clean tracker row.
