why we made this

we designed the katherine bra for runners who are tired of choosing between style and support. you know the feeling — you find a bra that holds up during speedwork, but it looks like something from a hospital catalog. or you find something cute and it's bouncing by mile two. the katherine fixes that. medium-high support that actually works for a-d cups, in colors you'll want to wear long after your cooldown.

  • 01

    medium-high support

    engineered for a-d cups with a wide, no-dig underband and adjustable racerback straps. stays locked in from first mile to last.

  • 02

    performance fabric

    moisture-wicking, 4-way stretch recycled nylon blend. dries twice as fast as cotton and moves with your body through every stride.

  • 03

    thoughtful design details

    removable padding for your preferred fit. smooth, bonded seams that won't chafe. flatlock stitching at every stress point.

  • 04

    made for your whole day

    running, hiit, strength training — and yes, post-run brunch. the kind of bra you forget you're wearing (in a good way).

  • 78% recycled nylon, 22% elastane
  • removable molded padding inserts
  • machine wash cold, gentle cycle
  • lay flat to dry — no tumble dry
  • do not iron or bleach
  • made ethically in portugal
