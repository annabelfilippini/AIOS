why we run

cooldown didn't start with a business plan. it started with a group chat. a few friends in denver who wanted to run together after work, grab a drink after, and actually talk to each other face-to-face.

turns out, a lot of people were craving the same thing. not another fitness app. not another virtual challenge. just a consistent place to show up, be yourself, and move with people who get it.

now we're in 15+ cities, and the formula hasn't changed: show up, run together, cool down together. no pace requirements, no membership fees, no ego. just people.

"the clothes came second. the community always came first."

— cooldown founders
