# Agency Audit Network Instructions

This project explores an audit-led referral network for AI automation agencies.

## Project Boundary

- Keep this project focused on the marketplace/referral model: client acquisition, audit product, agency partner network, pricing, validation, and operations.
- Use `projects/website-audit/` as adjacent execution inspiration, not as the source of truth for this business model.
- Store client lead notes in `prospects/`.
- Store agency partner notes in `partners/`.
- Store market/research notes in `research/`.
- Store repeatable intake, scoring, outreach, and audit templates in `research/templates/`.

## Strategic Defaults

- Reframe "agency monopoly" as "trusted audit and agency-matching network." The value is trust, clarity, and routing, not coercive control.
- Prefer manual concierge validation before building software.
- Treat the audit as the first product. Treat agency referrals as the second product.
- Separate client-facing claims from internal theories. Client-facing material should be specific, beautiful, and non-hypey.
- Do not promise implementation outcomes unless a partner agency has agreed to the scope, price, timeline, and responsibility.

## Compliance And Trust

- Do not scrape or use contact data in ways that violate platform terms, privacy expectations, CAN-SPAM, or other applicable rules.
- Outreach should be specific, honest, and easy to opt out of.
- Referral/kickback terms should be disclosed where appropriate and documented in writing with partner agencies.
- Avoid calling the model a monopoly in external materials.

## Current Adjacent Projects

- `projects/website-audit/` - manual-first website audit service and audit skills.
- `projects/consulting/` - AI operating systems consulting thesis and pilots.
