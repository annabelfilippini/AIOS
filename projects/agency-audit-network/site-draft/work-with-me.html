The Process

The audit maps the system your business can grow into.

The audit captures your business well enough to design an operating layer: durable context, clear workflows, human approval points, and components that can be queried and improved over time.

01

Context

We start by capturing how the business works: what you sell, who you serve, how you sound, where work lives, and what your team or agents need to know before helping.

02

Pods

Then we map the four pods: acquisition, delivery, support, and operations. This shows where the system should help first, and where automation would only make a messy process louder.

03

Architecture

You get a roadmap for the first version of your AIOS: what should become queryable, which workflows need guardrails, what can be delegated, and what should stay human-owned.

04

Iteration

The system should evolve as your business changes. You can build the first version yourself, bring in a vetted partner, and keep improving it through maintenance and health checks.
