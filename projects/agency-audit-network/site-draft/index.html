AF

This is my home base — who I am, what I am building, and how I can help. From here you can audit your company, find an AI consulting partner, or explore what I am working on.

I am a University of Michigan School of Information graduate, and what I really care about is connecting people with technology so the boring, repetitive work falls off their plate and they get more of their day back for the work that excites them. I want to help people take their businesses further than they thought possible — reach more people, and free up the space to finally start the side project, the dream job, or the outreach they never had time for.

For small businesses, I audit workflows and customer journeys to find where AI or better systems would help — and turn that into a clearer starting point. When the build is bigger than a starting point, I connect owners to AI consulting agencies I trust.

For AI consulting agencies, I work the other side of that handoff: shaping a clearer front door so they meet better-fit clients, with the need already scoped enough to make a referral worth trusting.

Alongside the client work, I share the thinking and experiments behind it — AI operating systems, research and workflow design, website audits, data storytelling, product judgment, and the small passion projects I am building to learn and grow in public.
