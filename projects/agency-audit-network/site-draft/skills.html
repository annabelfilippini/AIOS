AIOS Starting Point Audit

The system behind my audit work: scan acquisition, delivery, support, and operations; tag the friction; identify the keystone workflow; and turn the read into a clear next-step map.

Offer engine

Status: offer workflow from the agency audit project, not a standalone SKILL.md file.

How I work.

  • Read the real context. I start with the business, folder, workflow, or site as it exists, not with a generic AI use-case list.
  • Make the hidden workflow explicit. I name the tools, handoffs, repeat work, decision points, and places where quality or time leaks out.
  • Write down the smallest reusable system. A good skill is a repeatable path: clear enough for an agent, human enough for a person, and useful the next time the pattern appears.
  • Keep the judgment in the loop. External actions, client promises, sensitive data, spending, and irreversible changes stay human-approved.

These are the working systems behind how I think, research, audit, build, and review.
