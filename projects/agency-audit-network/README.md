# Agency Audit Network

Created: 2026-05-12

## One-Line Idea

Build a beautiful AI/business automation audit that helps companies understand what they should automate, then match the best-fit clients to vetted agencies and earn from both the audit and referral/partner revenue.

## Core Thesis

AI automation agencies are going to multiply quickly. Many will struggle to find qualified clients, price competitively, and prove they understand a business before pitching implementation.

This project positions Annabel as the trusted diagnostic and routing layer:

- Companies get a sharp, gorgeous audit of where AI and automation could improve their business.
- Agencies get warmer, better-scoped clients instead of chasing cold leads.
- Annabel earns from the audit itself and from successful agency referrals.

## What Makes It Interesting

Most businesses do not know what to ask an AI agency for. Most agencies would love to receive clients who already understand the opportunity, budget range, and implementation priorities.

The audit becomes the bridge. It turns vague AI curiosity into a prioritized scope that agencies can bid on or implement.

## First Customer Promise

"We will map where AI and automation could actually help your business, prioritize the highest-leverage opportunities, and introduce you to vetted implementation partners if you want help building it."

## Business Model

Initial revenue streams:

- Paid audit fee from the business.
- Referral fee or revenue share from the agency if the client signs.
- Optional premium matching fee for businesses that want help choosing between agencies.

Possible future revenue streams:

- Subscription dashboard for companies to track automation roadmap progress.
- Preferred partner network fees from agencies.
- Benchmark reports across industries or business types.
- White-labeled audit engine for agencies.

## Key Insight

Annabel should not start by becoming the implementation agency. The wedge is taste, trust, diagnosis, and routing. Implementation can stay partner-led until the demand pattern becomes obvious.
