Source: Authenticated capture of `skool.com/earlyaidopters/-/members` on 2026-05-12. Tom Filippini's account, level-sweep + admin-tab method.

**Coverage:** 199 of 1,400 total members captured. The captured set is the top slice by activity: all 16 admins, all level 6-7 members, and the top 30 (by recent activity) within each of levels 1-5. **Filter:** Implementation-operator-only — same as the Mansel v2 file. Bios are scored algorithmically against tool/verb/niche keywords; then I manually pulled in several high-activity members whose bios passed the implementation-shape sniff test but were under-caught by the keyword regex (notably "Founder of [AI agency name]" patterns).

**Caveat on scale:** Mark's community is a paid ($77/mo) implementation-focused community with explicit AI-builder positioning, so the *implementation-operator density is much higher* than Mansel's. Track 1 fit must still be checked per-candidate — many are vertical-specialized (mortgage, insurance, real estate, etc.) which doesn't match Cooldown-shape product brands but may fit v2/v3 tracks.

## Methodology / Future Scrapes

- **Pagination breakthrough:** Skool's `/members` page paginates server-side, but URL params `?p=` and `?page=` are ignored. **Working strategy:** `?levels=N` (1-9) returns up to 30 members per level, so a level-sweep + `?t=admin` tab captures the high-activity slice across a community of any size. For Mansel: 56/64 (88%). For Mark: 199/1,400 (top-activity slice; remaining 1,200 are level-1 lurkers and low-value for partner sourcing).
- **Bio enrichment:** Not needed — the level-sweep returns full bios in `__NEXT_DATA__.props.pageProps.users[].metadata.bio`.
- **Filter under-catches** noted: bare verbs ("I build" without an "I" preposition, "implement and build"), "Founder of [agency name]" patterns without an explicit tool. Manual review of WEAK-tier level-3+ members is worthwhile.
