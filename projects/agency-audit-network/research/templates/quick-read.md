## The opportunity

Most small product brands try to "add AI" — a chatbot here, a Klaviyo flow there. That treats AI like a tool.

A better way to think about it is to build an **AI Operating System for your business**: the set of systems that handle repeat work, free up your time, and let the business run without you touching every step.

Your business runs on four pods: **Acquisition** (how you get customers), **Delivery** (how you serve them), **Support** (how you keep them happy), and **Operations** (the back office). A good AIOS automates the right pieces of each — not every piece. This Quick Read sketches what the first version of your AIOS could look like. The full Deep Audit prioritizes by ROI, scopes effort, and gets you ready to either implement yourself or hand it to a vetted partner.

## Worked Example — Cooldown-like Product Brand

(Reference for what a filled Quick Read should look like. Delete this section in the actual client doc.)

### Components I'd build into your AIOS

**1. Automated post-purchase flow (Klaviyo + AI drafting)** · Delivery pod
- What it does: Sends order confirmation, shipping updates, review requests, replenishment reminders, and win-back sequences. AI drafts the copy for each new product so you're not writing emails by hand.
- Why this matters for you: Your post-purchase emails are the highest-margin content you're not sending consistently. Right now I see a confirmation email and not much else.
- Rough impact estimate: ~3 hours/week of your time + 8–15% lift in repeat purchase rate.

**2. Customer support triage agent** · Support pod
- What it does: Reads incoming emails/DMs, drafts replies based on order data + your past responses, flags anything sensitive for you to review.
- Why this matters for you: From your contact page, it looks like support comes to your personal email. That's a bottleneck and it scales poorly.
- Rough impact estimate: ~5 hours/week saved + faster reply times = higher CSAT.

**3. Returns + CX triage** · Support pod
- What it does: Auto-categorizes return reasons, drafts the apology/resolution message, surfaces patterns to you weekly (e.g., "size runs small" mentioned 12 times this month).
- Why this matters for you: Returns are where small product brands lose money quietly. Right now there's no system to see the trend.
- Rough impact estimate: 2 hours/week + protects margin by catching product issues early.

**4. Content + ad ops assistant** · Acquisition pod
- What it does: Drafts product descriptions, blog posts, ad copy variants, and IG captions from a shared brand voice file. You approve, edit, schedule.
- Why this matters for you: Content is the part of your business that scales your top of funnel. If only you can write in your voice, it doesn't scale.
- Rough impact estimate: 4 hours/week + meaningfully more content shipped per month.

**5. Inventory + ops alerts** · Operations pod
- What it does: Watches stock levels, sales velocity, supplier lead times. Drafts reorder POs. Alerts you when a SKU is about to go OOS.
- Why this matters for you: One stockout on your hero product probably costs more than this entire AIOS would.
- Rough impact estimate: 1–2 hours/week + prevents $X of stockout-driven revenue loss per quarter.
