# AIOS Starting Point Audit — Free Preview

Status: v1 draft — 2026-05-22
Purpose: give business owners a serious taste of the paid audit without collecting enough detail to replace the paid, agency-ready scoping brief.

## Positioning

The free audit should feel like a diagnostic preview, not a lead magnet quiz. It should help the user identify the most likely place their business is leaking time, money, quality, or capacity, then show why the paid audit goes deeper.

The free version should not ask for a full process map, Step Cards, QDOAA, ROI math, or agency handoff constraints. Those are the paid product.

## Recommended Question Set

Use 8 questions. This is enough to feel useful while staying light.

1. **What is your business name, industry, and rough size?**
   - Capture: company, industry, employee count or revenue band.

2. **Which part of the business feels most under pressure right now?**
   - Options: Acquisition, Delivery, Support, Operations.

3. **What is one workflow or process you wish worked better?**
   - Example prompt: lead follow-up, client onboarding, support triage, invoicing, weekly reporting.

4. **What usually goes wrong in that workflow?**
   - Options plus short explanation: takes too long, waits on people, errors/rework, bad data, unclear ownership, customer-visible issues, compliance/security risk.

5. **How often does this workflow happen?**
   - Options: daily, weekly, monthly, only during projects/campaigns, not sure.

6. **Who touches this workflow today?**
   - Capture roles, not necessarily names.

7. **What tools or systems are involved?**
   - Capture official tools plus spreadsheets/email/manual docs.

8. **If this workflow improved in the next 90 days, what would matter most?**
   - Options: save time, reduce errors, increase sales/conversion, improve customer experience, avoid risk, reduce team stress.

## Free Output

Return a short result with:

- **Likely audit area:** Acquisition / Delivery / Support / Operations.
- **Likely friction type:** time sink, wait/handoff, quality risk, data/compliance risk, open ownership loop.
- **Readiness note:** whether this sounds ready for automation, needs cleanup first, or needs better measurement.
- **Next best step:** paid audit if they want the workflow turned into Step Cards, ROI, Quick Wins, and an agency handoff brief.

## Paid Audit Teaser

The paid audit should be framed as the next layer:

- Free audit tells you **where to look**.
- Paid audit turns the workflow into **an agency-ready build brief**.
- Paid audit includes Step Cards, friction tags, QDOAA cleanup, ROI, Quick Wins, success criteria, and implementation constraints.
