1. **Frame the deliverable as the start of their AIOS.** A Quick Read becomes "the first sketch of your AI Operating System" rather than "a list of automations." This is a sharper hook than the playbook's neutral language.
2. **Use 4-engine scan as the lens** (Acquisition / Delivery / Support / Internal — from the audit playbook). The 4 engines map cleanly onto "what your AIOS would run."
3. **Talk in components, not tools.** Mansel sells systems, not Zaps. The Quick Read should list components ("automated post-purchase flow," "support triage agent") not vendor names.
4. **Outcome framing borrows from Mansel:** less time on repetitive work, more owner leverage, business that can run without the owner doing every step.
5. **Differentiates from generic AI agency pitches** (which sell isolated automations). Specificity-of-system is the wedge.
