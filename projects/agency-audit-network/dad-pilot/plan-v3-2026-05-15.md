## One-paragraph summary

Build the aviation AIOS as a company information and workflow system. **Tom and Erica both ship in Phase 1** on shared template workers — Tom's decision queue proves draft quality (he's technically fluent and uses Claude Code), and Erica's first scoped FSL workflow proves the non-technical-employee experience. **Tom and Josh are full co-owner seats** with complete company visibility, cross-seat rollups, approval rights, and access to system health. Source systems and the vault remain authoritative.

## Operating model

| Layer | Tooling | Purpose |
|---|---|---|
| Employee interface | Claude/Cowork | Daily queue, drafts, prep, approvals, scoped context |
| Owner interface | Claude/Cowork + owner views | Company-wide rollups, approvals, private/sensitive review |
| Company brain | Supabase | Operational state, access scopes, audit logs, lint findings |
| Hosted AIOS | Render | MCP/API service, scheduled jobs, validators, lint workers |
| Connectors | Composio + direct APIs | HubSpot, Front, Airtable, Microsoft 365, Slack, ART where available |
| Truth sources | Vault + source systems | Canon, customer records, trip/deal records, finance records |

Supabase is the shared operational brain. It is not the only source of truth. HubSpot, Front, ART, Airtable, Microsoft 365, finance systems, and the vault keep their authority; Supabase makes their information queryable, permissioned, summarized, audited, and usable inside Claude/Cowork.

## Access model

- **Tom and Josh:** full co-owner visibility across all companies, all queues, all lint findings, all workflow health, all audit logs, and all owner-only summaries.
- **Annabel/build-ops:** access to health checks, flagged validations, lint findings, implementation/debug context, and review queues. She does not become a permanent business owner of the system.
- **Erica:** scoped FSL seat. She sees her own queue, her own drafts, approved FSL context, FSL canon facts, and workflow health for her work. She does not see owner-only summaries, private finance, HR, Tom/Josh conversations, or other employees' queues.
- **Future employees:** same pattern as Erica: scoped by company, role, workflow, visibility, and sensitivity.

Default rule: information starts at the narrowest useful scope. It moves upward only when the workflow requires owner review, a human promotes it, or the AI flags risk involving money, legal, HR, customer escalation, strategy, or access.

## Information hygiene / lint layer

The system needs a lint layer so old information does not quietly poison new work.

Scheduled workers on Render:

- **Daily freshness lint:** finds expired drafts, stale queue items, duplicate signals, old briefs, missing source refs, and closed work still marked active.
- **Weekly conflict lint:** compares Supabase facts against vault/source systems and flags contradictions.
- **Monthly canon lint:** proposes canon updates from repeated edits, new approved facts, stale terms, and recurring employee corrections.
- **Connector drift lint:** catches sync failures, OAuth expiry, schema changes, missing cron runs, and unusual connector errors.
- **Access lint:** checks whether sensitive items are scoped too broadly or owner-only items are leaking into employee queues.

Default cleanup policy:

- Auto-archive low-risk stale items: expired drafts, duplicate signals, old queue items, resolved workflow records, and outdated brief rows.
- Never auto-delete or overwrite canon, CRM, finance, HR, customer commitments, source-system records, or access policies.
- Important stale/conflicting information becomes a review item for Tom/Josh or an approved admin.

## Supabase schema additions

Add tables for:

- `information_lint_runs`
- `stale_items`
- `conflict_flags`
- `cleanup_actions`
- `canon_update_candidates`

Add metadata to operational rows:

- `visibility`
- `sensitivity`
- `archived_at`
- `source_ref`
- `validation_status`
- `last_linted_at`

Every cleanup action is logged and reviewable. Low-risk archives must be reversible where the underlying row still exists.

## Test plan

- Erica cannot read owner-only rows, Tom/Josh private summaries, finance/HR/private items, or other employees' queues.
- Tom and Josh can read cross-company owner views, lint findings, cleanup history, and workflow health.
- A stale draft auto-archives and creates a `cleanup_actions` row.
- A conflicting fact flags into `conflict_flags` and does not overwrite source/canon automatically.
- A canon update candidate is proposed, not applied.
- A sensitive item scoped too broadly is caught by access lint.
- A connector failure appears in health checks and owner/admin views.
