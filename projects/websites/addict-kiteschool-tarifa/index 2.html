The spot

Why Tarifa

One of the best places in Europe to kite. Wide sandy beaches, warm weather, and steady wind almost all year. We pick the best spot on Los Lances each day for the conditions and your level, so you spend more time on the water.

Los Lances Beach

Wide, beginner friendly, room to learn safely.

Wind most of the year

Levante and Poniente keep Tarifa riding through the season.

From age 8

Kids welcome, with gear and pace adapted to them.
