## Services / offers / products

### Lessons — three formats (per person), 2026 season [verified, prices recheck before launch]

All three formats run 1–5 days with 2/3/4 hours per day. Pre-booking = -10%.

- **Group** — max 4 students per instructor, 2 kites (1 kite per 2 students).
  From **80€** (1 day, 3h). Pre-booked from 70€.
- **Semi-private** — max 2 students per instructor, 1 kite per student, radio
  helmet. From **120€** (1 day, 3h). Pre-booked from 110€.
- **Private** — 1 student per instructor, own kite, radio helmet. From **132€**
  (1 day, 2h). Pre-booked from 120€.

Every lesson includes: all equipment (kite, board, wetsuit, helmet), certified
instructor, insurance, rescue-boat assistance, and a level card. [verified]

Radio helmet (private + semi-private): instructor gives advice and instructions
live while the student is on the water. [verified]
