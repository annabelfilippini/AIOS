About Freeride

Freeride is an IKO affiliated kitesurfing school in Tarifa, Spain. Lessons, rental gear, kite stays and local guidance sit together so visitors can plan the whole wind week without guessing what belongs where.

2016 School founded in Tarifa
300+ Wind days around Tarifa each year
IKO Affiliated center with certified instructors
3 French, English and Spanish spoken

Location

Freeride teaches from the kite spots around Tarifa. The day is chosen around wind, tide and rider level, with Los Lances as the main beach.

Main spot
Los Lances

The main teaching beach for Freeride, close to Tarifa and used when the conditions fit the lesson level.

Second spot
Palmones

A protected alternative near Algeciras when the wind calls for a different teaching setup.

Third spot
Valdevaqueros

A classic Tarifa kite beach west of town, often used by schools when the wind window is right.
