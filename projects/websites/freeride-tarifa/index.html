Freeride Tarifa

Learn, ride, stay, and belong in Tarifa.

There is nothing quite like the freedom and thrill of kitesurfing, and we know it best. As true kite lovers, we travelled the best windy coasts in the world before setting up our school in one of the best kite spots in Europe, Tarifa. Lessons, rental gear, kite camps and yoga sit in one place, so you can plan the whole wind week.
