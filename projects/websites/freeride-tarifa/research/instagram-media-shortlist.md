## Read This First

Freeride's Instagram has much stronger website material than the current site:
real instructors, solo-traveler proof, beginner reassurance, Tarifa lifestyle,
kite camp atmosphere, and kite/yoga positioning.

Annabel especially likes the more human, close-up lesson photos: smiling
students, instructors beside students, people holding bars/boards, visible
Freeride rash guards/flags, and bright beach-water context. Prioritize these
over distant anonymous kite silhouettes.

Do not hotlink Instagram CDN image URLs in production. They are temporary and
can break. For final website use, either:

- ask Freeride for the original image/video files for the selected posts, or
- embed the Instagram posts/reels where a live social proof module is preferred.

For reels, the browser exposed full-resolution cover frames but not stable MP4
files. Treat reel videos as "request original clip from client" assets.

Use these as direction, not final production files. They are screenshots and
some include carousel UI/crops. Ask Freeride for the original images from these
posts or similar originals from the same shoot.

What to look for:

- close faces and visible emotion, especially first-win moments,
- student plus instructor together,
- Freeride-branded rash guards, flags, boards, or kite gear,
- hands-on coaching with the bar/lines visible,
- water action that still reads as attainable, not only expert tricks,
- warm sand, turquoise water, bright sky, and natural shallow depth of field.

Best website placements:

- Homepage: one happy student/instructor image near `About Freeride`.
- Instructors: real teacher/student moments rather than generic headshots only.
- Lessons: student looking up at the kite bar, coaching on the beach, first ride
  success.
- Options/Kite Camp: group or paired success images with gear.
- Brand/details: the Freeride flag image as a section break or footer visual.

## Website Direction From This Media

The site should feel less like a generic kite-school directory and more like:

- real people teaching you,
- a clear beginner-to-independent path,
- a kite camp and solo-traveler community,
- Tarifa as a full trip, not just a lesson location,
- kite plus yoga/wellness as a distinctive offer.

Immediate next move: replace generic/action-only imagery in the current static
preview with a mix of real instructor, student/camp, aerial location, town
lifestyle, and kite/yoga assets.
