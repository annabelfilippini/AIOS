Rent gear

Rent kitesurf equipment directly around the kite spot when you can ride upwind, recover your board and set up your kite safely. Freeride helps match the gear to the forecast before you go out.

Availability

April to October

Rental is organized around the season, the day’s forecast and the gear available for the rider.

Gear

Kite, bar and board

Choose a full pack, a kite and bar, or a board depending on what you need for the session.

Support

Forecast and spot advice

Freeride can help with kite size, launch conditions and rescue-card guidance on Levante days.

Rider level

Bring an IKO, VDWS or equivalent level card. If you do not have one, Freeride can check your level before confirming rental.

Ready to rent
  • Ride constantly upwind
  • Recover your board in body drag
  • Relaunch your kite from the water
  • Set up and launch your kite properly
  • Make transitions safely
Proof Level card

A certification card helps Freeride confirm that you can rent without a lesson.

Check 15 EUR

A personal level check can be added if you do not have a certification card.
