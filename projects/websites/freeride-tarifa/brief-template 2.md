# Project Brief Template

Fill this skeleton to produce a project-specific `design.md`. Each section has a
one-line instruction in italics describing what to write. Delete the italics in
the final brief. The structure mirrors the proven Freeride brief.

Read `brief-generator.md` for the step-by-step that produces these answers, and
`projects/websites/design.md` for the durable taste defaults this brief inherits.

---

## Visual System

*Specialize the taste file: shape language, palette (base + accents from real
photography), type (display + body), layout rhythm (dark/cinematic vs
pale/clear). Mandatory do/don'ts.*

## Copy Direction

*Voice in one line. 3-5 Good example lines in the business's real language. An

## Conversion

## Success Criteria

*4-6 criteria: what makes this rebuild objectively better than the current site
and better than category competitors.*

## Next Build Direction

*What to preserve from the current prototype and what to improve next. Keeps
iterations from restarting from scratch.*
