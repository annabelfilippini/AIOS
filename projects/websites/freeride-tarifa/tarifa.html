The wind capital of Europe

Tarifa

With its miles of windy beaches, unspoiled nature and countless bars and restaurants, Tarifa is known as the best kite spot in Europe. An ideal place to relax, have fun and learn kitesurfing.

Why Tarifa

The most reliable wind in Europe.

Tarifa enjoys over 300 days of excellent wind a year, making it the perfect place to practice kitesurfing. Two winds shape the season, the Levante from the east and the Poniente from the west, on long sandy beaches open to the Atlantic with a view of Morocco, just outside the village of Tarifa.

Where to ride

Three spots, one easy base

Tarifa is blessed with great stretches of pure white sand, all within a few minutes of the old town. Here is where you will be kiting, and which beach fits your level.

10 km of kite beach, all a short ride from town

1 Main kite spot Los Lances

The main kitesurf beach for schools, with wide flat sand and space to learn. This is where most lessons happen.

After kite: Agua or Waves beach bar
2 Wind and waves Valdevaqueros

A lively spot up the coast shared with windsurf schools, with strong wind on the big days.

Refuel: Tangana or Tumbao beach bar
3 Closest to town Balneario

A relaxed spot facing the ocean, right by the old town to unwind after a session.

Relax: Waikiki or Long Beach
