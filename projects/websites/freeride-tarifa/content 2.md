Course roadmap [verified]:
- Step 1 Discovery (~4 hrs): kite control, safety, wind direction, gear setup,
  launch and landing.
- Step 2 Intermediate A (~3 hrs): body dragging, power stroke, moving through the
  water without the board.
- Step 3 Intermediate B (~6 hrs): water start, first rides, board handling,
  consistency.
- Step 4 Independent (ongoing): consistent riding, upwind work, supervision,
  advanced progression.
