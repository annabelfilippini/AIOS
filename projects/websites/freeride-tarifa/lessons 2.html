Course roadmap

The course starts with kite control because most early progress depends on reading the wind, handling power and staying safe before the board becomes the focus.

Step 1 · about 4 hours Discovery

Kite control, safety, wind direction, gear setup, launch and landing.

Step 2 · about 3 hours Intermediate A

Body dragging, power stroke and moving through the water without the board.

Step 3 · about 6 hours Intermediate B

Water start, first rides, board handling and building consistency.

Step 4 · ongoing Independent

Consistent riding, upwind work, supervision and advanced progression.

IKO certification syllabus

The IKO levels give riders a clear progression from first kite handling through independent and advanced riding.

IKO levels

Freeride lists four IKO stages from first kite handling through advanced riding.

IKO Lv.1 Discovery

Introduction to kitesurfing, kite control and safety.

IKO Lv.2 Intermediate

Body drag, power control and preparation for first rides.

IKO Lv.3 Independent

Consistent riding, confidence and safer independent practice.

IKO Lv.4 Advanced

Technical progression, tricks and stronger rider control.
