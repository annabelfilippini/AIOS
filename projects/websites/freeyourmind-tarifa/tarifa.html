The destination

Tarifa

The southern tip of Spain, where the Atlantic meets the Mediterranean and you can see Africa across the strait. One of the best wind and surf cities in the world.

No wind, no pay · full refund, every time

Why Tarifa

Pristine waters and lush nature

You will enjoy the pristine waters and lush nature of Tarifa and its surroundings, relax on the golden sandy beaches of the Costa de la Luz, and discover the famous nightlife of the old town and its chiringuitos. It is one of the reasons we founded Free your Mind here, on one of the best wind cities and surf spots in the world.

Whether you are a beginner or an advanced rider, coming alone, with a partner, family or friends, there is always plenty to do, on and off the water.

Costa
de la Luz

Long sandy beaches, turquoise water, and the steady wind of the strait. From beginner bays to the famous Valdevaqueros and the open Atlantic, there is a spot for every level and every condition.
