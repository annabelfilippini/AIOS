Welcome

What to expect on your kitesurfing holidays in Tarifa

You will enjoy the pristine waters and lush nature of Tarifa and its surroundings. Relax on the golden sandy beaches of the Costa de la Luz and discover the famous nightlife of Tarifa town and its chiringuitos. When there is no wind there is yoga, wakeboarding, horse riding, stand-up paddle and surfing. We are specialists in tailor made trips.
