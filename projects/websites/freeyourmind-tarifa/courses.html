How we teach

Small groups, real progress

You will meet a team of trained and qualified instructors, certified to IKO, VDWS and FAV standards, who teach in a responsible and motivated way. Our courses are taught in English, German, French and Spanish.

We place special emphasis on small groups, sometimes just two students, and also offer the normal four-person group. The fewer people sharing the kite, the faster you get on the board. We provide all the necessary and complete kitesurfing equipment, as well as insurance and safety boats.
