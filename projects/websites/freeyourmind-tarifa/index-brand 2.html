About Free Your Mind

Free Your Mind was born from the wind, the sea, and a desire to live each day as if it was the first.

Tanja Rosenkranz spent years competing and teaching kitesurfing all over the world. After almost a decade as an instructor, she wanted to make something bigger than a school. A space where sport, travel and real connection with people would mix naturally.

Today Free Your Mind is a community. An international team eager to share what they love, in a place where the important thing is not what we do, but why we do it.

The location

Kitesurfing in Tarifa

At the point where the Atlantic meets the Mediterranean, facing Africa, the wind blows all year and the beaches never seem to end. When there is no wind, there are mountains, paths, and the chance to cross to Africa in an hour.

Our base is in Los Lances Norte, reachable even without a car. And if the wind changes, we change with it and take you to the best spot of the day.
