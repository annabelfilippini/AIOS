Accommodation

From our own villa to town

Whether you prefer the heart of the old town, a place on the beach or somewhere quiet in the countryside, we will find the best option for your holiday. From our own Free your Mind Kite Villa to studios, apartments and hotels in Tarifa, with the kitesurf lifestyle built in.

If none of the options below are quite what you are looking for, we are happy to help you find that special place. Just tell us what you need.

La Vega Studios

$$

A conversion of an old cortijo, four open plan studio apartments around an open air courtyard, all with a cosy yet modern, fresh design. Enjoy the shared courtyard for a meal, a barbecue or a good old sunbathing session. Free parking and somewhere to wash and store your equipment. You can also gather friends or family and take two, three or all four studios together.

Valdevaqueros Hotel

$$

Near the famous Valdevaqueros beach, one of Tarifa's best loved kite spots. Ask us about rooms and current availability.

Ask about the hotel

La Residencia Puerto SPA Hotel

$$$

The comfort of a four-star hotel in the very heart of Tarifa, between the harbour and the historic old town, close to the beaches and restaurants. Fifty rooms, a spa with hammam and sauna, a rooftop pool and a small restaurant, El Patio, serving traditional cuisine with Arabic influences. The rooms combine Moroccan touches with natural materials, friendly and relaxed.

Ask about the hotel

La Residencia Apartments

$$$

Sixteen apartments in a historic building right at the port and the old town, within the old city wall. Most apartments overlook the Strait of Gibraltar and Africa. On the roof terrace there is a heated pool, a sauna and a large sun terrace.
