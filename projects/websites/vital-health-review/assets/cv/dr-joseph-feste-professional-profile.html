Professional Summary

Dr. Joseph R. Feste is the founder of Vital Health Integrative Medicine and a board-certified OBGYN. The current Vital Health site describes him as no longer seeing patients and serving the practice through administrative guidance, research, insight, and educational leadership.

Public professional listings describe his medical training at Baylor College of Medicine, residency training at Baylor University Medical Center, and board certification in Obstetrics and Gynecology. Published JSLS author information lists Dr. Feste as a Clinical Associate Professor in the Department of Obstetrics and Gynecology at Baylor College of Medicine and the University Health Science Center at Houston.
