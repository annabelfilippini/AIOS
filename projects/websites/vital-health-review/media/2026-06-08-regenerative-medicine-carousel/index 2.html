Definition

A category of care, not one protocol.

Regenerative medicine is a clinical lane that supports the body's own healing and repair. It is shaped by your goals, your history, and your labs.

May be considered when clinically appropriate.

When it may be considered

Where the conversation usually starts.

  • Recovery from injury or procedures
  • Joint, tendon, and connective tissue care
  • Persistent fatigue and slow recovery
  • Hormonal and metabolic support during healing
