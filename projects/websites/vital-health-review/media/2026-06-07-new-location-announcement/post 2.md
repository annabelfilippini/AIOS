## Direction

One-off location announcement for Vital Health. This is not a medical education post. The design should feel calm, clinical, warm, and useful, with the new address treated as the main message.

The post uses Vital Health's forest, cream, sage, gold, terracotta, and paper palette. It uses the real hummingbird mark and a quiet map-inspired visual instead of a generic clinic photo or fake building image.
