## Slide copy

1. **Cover.** What are peptides? · Vital Health can help you decide if they fit your care plan.
2. **Definition.** Peptides are short chains of amino acids. In the body, some act as messengers that help cells and tissues communicate.
3. **Why personal.** The same peptide conversation means a different thing depending on the patient. Age, hormones, sleep, immune history, medications, and metabolic markers all matter. Vital Health starts with the full clinical picture, not a trend.
4. **Examples patients may hear about.** Names like Sermorelin, BPC-157, Thymosin alpha-1, and PT-141 come up in patient conversations. These are examples, not recommendations. What is clinically appropriate is decided one patient at a time.
5. **How to ask Vital Health.** Bring your goals, your history, and your questions. Complimentary 60 minute consultation. No cost. No commitment.
