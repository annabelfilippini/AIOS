## Caption draft

When are peptides considered?

Peptides are short chains of amino acids that can act as messengers in the body. At Vital Health, we treat them as a category of options inside a full clinical picture, not a menu to choose from.

Whether a peptide may be considered depends on indication, history, labs, route, dose, and how we plan to measure progress. Some patients ask about peptides for recovery and tissue repair. Some ask in the context of growth hormone axis support, when clinically indicated. Some ask about immune support after a long illness. In every case, it starts with the same conversation: what is going on, what have we measured, and what is actually appropriate for you.

If you have a question about peptides, bring your goals, your history, and your questions to a complimentary 60 minute consultation. No cost. No commitment.

Educational only. Not medical advice. Peptide therapy should be discussed with a licensed clinician.
