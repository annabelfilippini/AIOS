Hormone Optimization

Bio-identical hormone therapy for men and women, the long view of a hormone picture, tested quarterly and adjusted at micro-dose precision.

Book a consultation →

Hormones touch nearly everything. If you are struggling with weight, sleep, energy, libido, migraines, depression, hot flashes, night sweats, or difficult periods, your hormones are likely involved.

For women

Vital Health specializes in hormone replacement therapy with bio-identical hormones. Women spend a third to half of their lives in the post-menopausal phase, often under-treated, and precisely our specialty.

Testosterone alone can decline by as much as 50% between the ages of 20 and 50. Bioidentical therapy helps protect post-menopausal women against:

  • Heart disease
  • Dementia and Alzheimer's disease
  • Urinary incontinence
  • Collagen loss and decreased skin elasticity

Post-menopausal women may also experience:

  • Fatigue and disrupted sleep
  • Weight gain and metabolic changes
  • Vaginal dryness and painful intercourse
  • Mood changes, anxiety, and depression
  • Brain fog and memory lapses
  • Joint pain and decreased bone density
  • Reduced libido
  • Hair thinning and skin changes

Hormonal imbalances do not only affect older women. Adolescents and young adults experiencing PMS, thyroid issues, PCOS, or infertility are also candidates for careful hormonal support.

For men

Testosterone, DHEA, thyroid, and cortisol are interdependent. A shift in one can reshape the others. Men's hormone optimization at Vital Health begins with comprehensive labs and targets lean muscle, libido, cognitive function, cardiovascular protection, and long horizon vitality.

Men with low testosterone may experience:

  • Fatigue and low energy
  • Reduced libido and sexual dysfunction
  • Loss of lean muscle mass
  • Increased body fat, particularly around the abdomen
  • Brain fog and difficulty concentrating
  • Mood changes, irritability, and depression
  • Poor sleep and insomnia
  • Decreased bone density
  • Hair thinning or loss
  • Reduced motivation and drive
  • Cardiovascular changes and decreased stamina

What we treat

  • Low testosterone
  • Perimenopause & post-menopause
  • Thyroid dysfunction
  • Hot flashes & night sweats
  • PCOS & cycle dysregulation
  • Weight & metabolism
  • Sleep & energy
  • Mood, brain fog & libido
Your first visit is 60 minutes. You leave with a testing panel, a named practitioner, and a protocol written from your labs and clinical picture, not assumption.

Peptide therapy works best when it's personal. At Vital Health, every treatment is guided by your labs and goals, recommended only when your full clinical picture supports it.

How peptide therapy is considered

Peptides are naturally occurring biological molecules found in all living organisms. They function as biologic messengers carrying information from one tissue through the blood to another, and may be considered only when a patient's goals, labs, and clinical history support that direction.

Protocols we may discuss

Exosomes, explained

Exosomes are extracellular vesicles generated by cells in the body. They carry nucleic acids, proteins, lipids, and metabolites, and are studied for their role as cellular messengers involved in inflammation signaling and tissue repair pathways.

How we deliver them

IV Push

Systemic distribution targeting inflammation throughout the body.

Atomizer

Nasal mist delivery emphasizing cognitive and neurological concerns: brain fog, TBI, stroke recovery.

Nebulizer

Inhalation via face mask, targeting lungs and sinuses for respiratory support.

Direct Injection

Localized delivery into specific joints or tissue areas for targeted repair.

IV therapy & ozone protocols

Vital Health's IV and ozone offerings deserve their own spotlight inside regenerative medicine. Protocols may include hydration and nutrient IVs, Myers' cocktail, NAD+, glutathione, targeted ozone therapy, and additional routes discussed privately when clinically appropriate.

Advanced Diagnostics & Early Detection

Preventive testing for genetics, cancer signals, biological age, cognition, cardiovascular risk, gut health, and exposure history.

Book a consultation →

The future of medicine is anticipatory. At Vital Health, we offer a suite of advanced diagnostic tools that go far beyond standard labs, giving you a clearer, earlier, and more actionable picture of your health.

Testing we may review

DNA Testing

Your genetics are a roadmap. DNA testing can reveal inherited risks, metabolic tendencies, and medication sensitivities that shape clinical decisions from hormone dosing to nutrition and long-term prevention.

Galleri Multi-Cancer Early Detection

Galleri is a blood test that screens for signals associated with more than 50 types of cancer, often before symptoms appear. We discuss it as part of preventive care when the patient's risk profile supports it.

GlycanAge

Biological age and chronological age are not the same. GlycanAge measures immune system age through glycan biomarkers, adding another lens to how the body is aging and what can be changed.

NexGen

Next-generation sequencing maps health at the molecular level, helping identify risks and patterns that conventional testing may miss.

Cognivue

Cognitive health deserves the same early attention as cardiovascular or metabolic health. Cognivue is an FDA-cleared assessment used to establish a baseline and track changes over time.

Carotid Ultrasound

Cardiovascular imaging considered when vascular risk and prevention need a closer look.

HRV & Body Composition

Metrics that can help track recovery, resilience, muscle, fat distribution, and metabolic progress.

Heavy Metal Testing

Screening reviewed when exposure history or symptoms suggest it may be useful.

GI MAP

Gut health testing used when digestion, inflammation, or microbiome questions are part of the picture.

Full Body MRI Offering

An added screening option the clinic is evaluating for deeper investigation when appropriate.

These tools do not replace clinical judgment. At Vital Health, advanced diagnostics are integrated into your care, not offered as an afterthought.
