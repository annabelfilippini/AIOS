The team

Coached by people who live for the wind

Small classes, real attention, and instructors who would rather be on the water than anywhere else. Three or four students per kite in a group, fewer in a duo or private, so you actually get coached, not queued.

Founder · Instructor Roel Oude Avenhuis

Born in Aruba, where his family runs the largest watersport business in the Caribbean. Grew up surfing, sailing and kiting, and is as good at motivating people as he is at reading the wind.

Instructor Lex Donse

A real wind junkie who is at his best above 6 Beaufort in big waves. Years of teaching in every condition mean he can explain the hard parts simply.

Instructor Marcus van Dijk

Chases summer around the planet, then brings that energy back to the Dutch coast. Cheerful, spontaneous and patient, he will have you on the board before you expect it.

The spot

One base, three kinds of water

We are based at Kijkduin, the quieter beach next to Scheveningen on the edge of The Hague. Our beach club Boca Grandi is home, with free showers, changing rooms and a place to eat after the session. From here the wind decides which water you ride.

The Zandmotor

Flat · shallow · learn here

A sheltered lagoon where you can stand up. The calmest, safest water to take your first lessons, and where we head whenever the wind blows offshore.

Kijkduin beach

Home base · free parking

Right in front of Boca Grandi. Park for free on Machiel Vrijenhoeklaan, walk a few steps, and you are on the sand with your gear.

Scheveningen Strand Zuid

Open sea · waves · advanced

The designated watersport zone for stronger riders who want real sea and waves. This is where wave and advanced sessions move.
