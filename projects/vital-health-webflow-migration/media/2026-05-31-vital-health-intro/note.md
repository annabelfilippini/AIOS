## The 6-slide arc

| # | Role  | Move                                          | Headline beat                                |
|---|-------|-----------------------------------------------|----------------------------------------------|
| 1 | hero  | full-bleed human + identity wordmark          | category claim ("medicine that catches it…") |
| 2 | body  | split-left-text + botanical still-life        | origin / why this exists                     |
| 3 | body  | full-bleed centered + styled artifact UI      | first-visit specifics (90 min)               |
| 4 | body  | typographic emphasis on one word              | what's actually different (labs / record)    |
| 5 | body  | warm photo of the founder or team             | who you'd actually be working with           |
| 6 | cta   | quiet close + single concrete next step       | the call-to-action (free 30-min call)        |

## What to lift

- **Arc shape** — hero → origin → process → differentiator → people → cta.
- **Photography variety** — each slide uses a *different* composition system
  (full-bleed, split, still-life, founder portrait). Never six near-identical panels.
- **Voice** — concrete, plain, no marketing hype. "Ninety minutes" not "comprehensive."
- **One-word emphasis** on body slides (italic display serif on the key word).
- **CTA is one sentence + the URL.** No stacked buttons or hype.

## What NOT to lift

- The exact copy (it's Vital Health's, not template language).
- The exact font pairing (Fraunces / Inter). Replace with the brand's actual fonts.
- The hummingbird logo, color palette, Austin references, founder name.
