# Vital Health Voice Profile

## Summary

Vital Health sounds calm, established, precise, and human. The voice should feel like a long-trusted Austin integrative medicine practice: warm enough to feel relational, restrained enough to feel medically credible, and clear enough that a patient understands what to do next.

## Tone

- Quiet confidence, not hype.
- Clinical grounding, not sterile jargon.
- Whole-body care, not miracle claims.
- Relationship-first, not funnel-first.
- Editorial and refined, not social-media loud.

## Vocabulary

Use:
- integrative care
- whole-body health
- clinical foundation
- long view
- measured
- physician-supervised
- prescribed against your physiology
- care that reads like a relationship
- built on legacy, built for you

Avoid:
- miracle
- cure
- guaranteed
- reverse disease
- melt fat
- biohack
- anti-aging promise language
- urgency/scarcity
- before-and-after medical claims

## Social Rules

- Instagram carousels should teach one small idea at a time.
- Keep slide copy short and calm.
- Prefer "what to expect" and "how we think about care" over aggressive problem agitation.
- If a service is mentioned, avoid outcome promises. Frame it as an evaluated clinical option.
- End with a soft next step: consultation, ask questions, or learn more.

## Example Lines

- "A different kind of integrative care."
- "Your first visit is a full workup, not a triage."
- "Protocols written against your labs, not assumption."
- "Care here reads more like a relationship than an appointment."
- "The long view of whole-body health."
