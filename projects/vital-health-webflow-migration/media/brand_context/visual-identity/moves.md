# Vital Health Design Moves

## Editorial Masthead

<!--meta name="editorial-masthead" image_bearing=false keywords=["masthead", "kicker", "top label"] -->

Tiny all-caps labels across the top edge, separated by gold dots. Used to make social posts feel established and periodical-like.

## Forest Emphasis Word

<!--meta name="forest-emphasis-word" image_bearing=false keywords=["headline", "green", "serif"] -->

Large serif headings in ink with one important word set in forest green.

## Botanical Still Life

Soft clinical still-life imagery with green leaves, amber glass, capsules, linen, marble, and warm natural shadows.

## Gold Micro Rule

<!--meta name="gold-micro-rule" image_bearing=false keywords=["rule", "divider", "pulse", "accent"] -->

Thin gold line work, small pulse marks, and understated dividers.

## Hummingbird Trust Mark

<!--meta name="hummingbird-trust-mark" image_bearing=true required_zone_types=["logo"] keywords=["logo", "hummingbird", "trust mark"] -->

The hummingbird line mark appears small, usually near the footer or closing slide.

## Carousel Hero — Human Touch + Centered Serif

<!--meta name="carousel-hero-human-touch" image_bearing=true required_zone_types=["photo"] keywords=["cover", "hero", "human", "touch", "serif", "centered"] -->

Cover slide. Soft full-bleed human-moment photo (hands touching, a calm clinical gesture, a patient detail) with a centered two-line Fraunces serif headline stacked under a thin gold vertical micro-rule. One muted-cream subline at the bottom. Inspired by Function Health's "Gift the only thing that matters" cover, translated to the cream/forest/gold system.

## Carousel Artifact Slide — Real Vital Health UI

<!--meta name="carousel-artifact-ui" image_bearing=true required_zone_types=["screenshot", "ui"] keywords=["intake", "form", "scheduling", "report", "proof", "interface"] -->

Body slide that shows a REAL Vital Health artifact: an intake form excerpt, a scheduling calendar, a sample lab snippet, a portal screenshot. Floats on cream ground with a thin gold rule + masthead. Anchors credibility — the carousel must show what working with the practice actually looks like, not just talk about it. Borrows the "show the actual interface" move from the Oura Ring ad.

## Carousel Patient Quote — Italic Serif + Gold Signature Rule

<!--meta name="carousel-patient-quote" image_bearing=false keywords=["quote", "italic", "testimonial", "patient", "signature"] -->

Body slide. A single patient or practitioner sentence set in Fraunces italic serif, centered or left-aligned on cream or forest-green ground. Attribution sits below a thin gold rule, e.g. "— J.M., patient since 2022" in small Inter caps. NO product render, no promo intensity. Restrained translation of Bloom Nutrition's quote-card to a service brand.

## Carousel Credential Slide — Pull-Quote Trust Mark

<!--meta name="carousel-credential-trust" image_bearing=false keywords=["credential", "trust", "press", "pull quote", "authority"] -->

Body slide. ONE large serif credential or press pull-quote (e.g. "Board-certified Integrative MD" or "15 years serving Austin families") set big, centered, on cream or paper ground. Attribution or source under a thin gold rule. Inspired by the Solawave "Best LED Face Mask — Vogue" treatment, with the credential format dialed to service-medical restraint.

## Carousel Loud Slide — Stat on Still-Life

<!--meta name="carousel-loud-stat" image_bearing=true required_zone_types=["photo", "still-life"] keywords=["stat", "fact", "loud", "botanical", "headline"] -->

Body slide — used ONCE per carousel, max. A real Vital Health-relevant fact (years in practice, average consult length, hours per patient) set in cream Fraunces serif, sized large, over a botanical or clinical still-life photo from the existing image library. The lesson from Plunge: one slide is allowed to be loud. Avoid condensed sans, percentage-claim copy, and anything that reads as marketing-claim.

## Carousel Sign-Off — Mirror of Hero

<!--meta name="carousel-signoff-cta" image_bearing=true required_zone_types=["photo"] keywords=["cta", "sign-off", "closing", "book", "consult"] -->

Final slide. Mirrors the hero composition (human-touch photo + centered Fraunces serif + thin gold rule) but the headline becomes a quiet CTA: "Book a consult", "Visit us in Austin", "Schedule with Vital Health". Hummingbird trust mark sits small near the footer. The carousel closes the way it opened, signaling care.

## One Micro-Rule Per Slide

<!--meta name="one-micro-rule-per-slide" image_bearing=false keywords=["rule", "divider", "gold", "structure"] -->

Every slide carries exactly one gold horizontal or vertical micro-rule, 1-2px, used structurally (under a signature, between headline and subline, separating masthead from body). Never decorative pattern, never two rules on the same slide. This is what keeps the carousel feeling like a single editorial sequence rather than disconnected panels.

## Carousel Layout Diversity (Anti-Sameness)

<!--meta name="carousel-layout-diversity" image_bearing=false keywords=["diversity", "variety", "composition", "carousel"] -->

No two slides in a single carousel share a layout template. If slide 2 uses a centered headline over photo, slide 3 must use left-aligned text on cream with no photo. If slide 4 is a quote card, slide 5 must be a stat-on-photo. This is the cure for the paused-pilot failure mode where six near-identical green panels read as "branded but lifeless".
