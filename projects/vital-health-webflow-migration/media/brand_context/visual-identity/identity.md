# Vital Health Visual Identity

Vital Health's visual identity is a calm editorial medical-wellness system: cream paper fields, deep forest green type, gold accent rules, refined serif headlines, quiet clinical still-life imagery, and a light botanical motif anchored by the hummingbird logo.

## Core Moves

- Cream or paper backgrounds with generous margins.
- Fraunces-style serif display type with selective forest-green emphasis.
- Small all-caps masthead labels with wide letter spacing.
- Thin gold rules, dots, or pulse-line accents.
- Botanical still-life images: leaves, glass, amber bottles, capsules, linen, natural light.
- Square or minimally rounded CTAs.
- Hummingbird/logo as a soft trust marker, not a loud watermark.

## Social Carousel Direction

Instagram posts should feel like a cropped editorial page from the website: minimal, warm, useful, and medically restrained. Use images as atmosphere and trust, not as loud illustration. Most slides should have one idea, one headline, and a small supporting sentence.

Carousel rhythm follows six slide roles (catalog in `moves.md`):

1. **Hero / cover** — human-touch photo + centered serif over a gold micro-rule (Function-style, translated to cream/forest/gold)
2. **Artifact / proof** — a real Vital Health UI element (intake form, scheduling calendar, sample report)
3. **Patient quote** — italic serif sentence + signature on cream or forest ground
4. **Credential / trust** — single large serif credential or pull-quote under a thin gold rule
5. **One loud slide** — a single relevant stat in cream Fraunces serif over a botanical or clinical still-life photo (used once per carousel, max)
6. **Sign-off / CTA** — mirror of the hero, headline becomes "Book a consult" or "Visit us in Austin"

Hard rule: no two slides share a layout template. The 2026-05-31 inspo merge (refs: Oura, Function Health, Bloom Nutrition, Solawave, Plunge — analyzed in `_refs/inspo-notes.md`) is the reason this rhythm exists. Without role variety the carousel collapses into "six identical green panels" — the paused-pilot failure mode.
