# Vital Health Writing Samples

## Website Hero

"Your journey to optimal vitality."

Why it works: aspirational but restrained, with the strongest word carried by the brand green.

## Practice Positioning

"A different kind of integrative care."

Why it works: concise, calm, and differentiated without sounding combative.

## Philosophy

"A practice built on legacy. A future built on you."

Why it works: connects the founder history with current patient-centered care.

## Care Model

"Care here reads more like a relationship than an appointment."

Why it works: captures warmth and slowness without overclaiming.

## Clinical Process

"A full workup, not a triage."

Why it works: short, memorable, and clinically grounded.
