# Vital Health Positioning

Vital Health is an established Austin integrative medicine practice for patients who want thoughtful, whole-body care with medical supervision and a long clinical foundation.

## Differentiation

- Over 50 years of clinical foundation through Dr. Joseph Feste's legacy.
- Integrative, regenerative, and preventive care under one practice.
- Physician-supervised programs and lab-aware protocols.
- Relationship-based care with longer first visits and measured follow-through.

## Core Frame

Vital Health is not a quick-fix wellness brand. It is a calm, clinically grounded practice for patients who want to understand their physiology and make considered decisions with a care team.

## Social Content Angle

Teach the thinking behind the practice:
- why first visits are longer
- how labs inform decisions
- what integrative care means in plain English
- when hormone, peptide, weight-loss, and rejuvenation services are evaluated
- how patients can prepare for a consultation
