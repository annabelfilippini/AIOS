# Vital Health ICP

## Primary Audience

Adults in the Austin area who are actively looking for a more comprehensive medical relationship than a standard short appointment. They may be dealing with energy, hormones, weight, longevity, recovery, or general whole-body health concerns, but they need a credible medical team rather than wellness hype.

## What They Want

- Someone to look at the whole picture.
- A longer first conversation.
- Labs and medical context before recommendations.
- Clear next steps without pressure.
- A practice that feels established, not trendy.

## What They Are Skeptical Of

- Overpromising.
- Generic supplement or hormone protocols.
- Influencer-style wellness advice.
- Medical weight loss without clinical supervision.
- Vague "optimize everything" language.

## Language That Resonates

- "full workup"
- "physician-supervised"
- "adjusted against your labs"
- "whole-body health"
- "measured"
- "long-term"
- "not a protocol pulled off a shelf"
