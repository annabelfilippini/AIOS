# Functional Health Inspiration For Vital Health

Research date: 2026-05-31

Use this as a swipe file for Vital Health social ads, website polish, and future promo pages. The best direction is not full sci-fi. It is Vital Health's calm editorial trust system with a thin layer of modern diagnostic clarity: biomarkers, lab panels, app-like insight cards, clean medical diagrams, and measured copy.

## Best Fit For Vital Health

### Westlake Medical Arts

- URL: https://www.westlakemedicalarts.com/
- Why it matters: Austin-based, functional and integrative, warm clinical tone, botanical motifs, physician credibility.
- Swipe: "rooted in science, guided by compassion" style positioning; simple leaf illustration; human provider trust.
- Vital Health use: closest local/practice reference, but Vital Health should look more premium and established.

### Etanah Medicine And Wellness

- URL: https://www.etanah.com/
- Why it matters: quiet concierge longevity positioning with simple, editorial copy.
- Swipe: sparse homepage language, "Thoughtful. Intentional. Designed around you." rhythm, restrained service groupings.
- Vital Health use: good model for softer luxury without sounding like a med spa.

### Parsley Health

- URL: https://www.parsleyhealth.com/company
- Why it matters: physician-led functional medicine with whole-body rigor, protocols, tracking, and technology-enabled care.
- Swipe: explain why clinicians are different; make functional medicine feel systemized and medically serious.
- Vital Health use: translate "functional" from vague wellness into clear clinical method.

### Eclipse Wellness

- URL: https://eclipsewellnessclinic.com/
- Why it matters: modern longevity language with a more reflective, whole-person frame.
- Swipe: "trajectory" language, aging as function and alignment rather than youth-chasing.
- Vital Health use: helpful for the long-view / follow-through story.

## Vital Health Creative Direction From This

### Keep

- Cream paper, forest green, gold rules, serif headlines, botanical still-life.
- Calm authority: "measured, personal, grounded."
- Provider and founder legacy as credibility.
- Clinical specificity: labs, hormones, peptides, weight loss, regenerative care.

### Add

- Small diagnostic UI cards: biomarker ranges, lab review steps, care-plan checkpoints.
- Thin line diagrams: "history -> labs -> goals -> plan -> follow-through."
- More modern service ads: one symptom, one clinical lens, one low-pressure consult CTA.
- Subtle futurism: data clarity, precision, better tracking, not neon cyber-health.

### Avoid

- Aggressive anti-aging promises.
- Biohacker bro copy.
- Overly sterile black/blue tech palettes.
- Before/after weight-loss ad energy.
- Claim-heavy copy that could create medical-review headaches.
