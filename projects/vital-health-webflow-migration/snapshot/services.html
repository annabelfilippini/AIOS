Peptide Therapy

A treatment method that stimulates cellular regrowth systems — targeting longevity, recovery, and vitality at the messenger level.

Book a consultation →

Peptides are naturally occurring biological molecules found in all living organisms. They function as biologic messengers carrying information from one tissue through the blood to another.

How peptide therapy works

Peptides operate by attaching to receptors on the surface of cells. This allows them to provide cells and molecules with instructions for specific actions. The therapy works epigenetically — turning off genes that manifest disease and turning on longevity promoting genes. Peptides jumpstart the healing process because they interact with hundreds of healing pathways in the body.

Regenerative medicine at Vital Health focuses on utilizing advanced therapies to restore function, repair tissues, and enhance overall well-being. The cornerstone of this work is exosome therapy.

Exosomes, explained

Exosomes are extracellular vesicles generated by all cells in the body. They carry nucleic acids, proteins, lipids, and metabolites — functioning as cellular messengers that address inflammation and tissue repair throughout the body. These healing agents become less abundant with age, declining from approximately 96% stem cell concentration at birth to just 7% by age 80.
