# Spent

Small native iPhone app for logging purchases quickly.

## Product Direction

- Keep the app tiny: amount, item, date, totals, recent entries.
- Prioritize fast entry over budgeting complexity.
- White, clean, calm visual design.
- Avoid accounts, analytics, cloud sync, or financial integrations unless explicitly requested.

## Technical Direction

- Native SwiftUI app.
- Local-only storage first.
- Treat TestFlight sharing as the first distribution path.
