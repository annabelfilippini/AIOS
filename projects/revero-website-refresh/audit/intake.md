## Annabel Says

Initial inferred quality bar from prior projects:

- The site should feel clinically credible, calm, premium, and human.
- The root-cause positioning should become clearer without sounding like a
  miracle-cure funnel.
- The design should reduce repeated sections and noisy CTAs.
- Medical proof should feel more trustworthy, not more hyped.

## Reality Says

The current site has a strong offer and a real differentiation story, but it
looks like a conversion funnel that has accumulated old sections, duplicate
mobile/desktop blocks, grammar mistakes, and high-risk medical claims.

The first process decision is not "make it prettier." It is: clean up trust,
claims, structure, and conversion hierarchy before redesigning visuals.
