About us

Revero is a online medical clinic treating chronic health conditions with personalized medical treatments and precision nutrition. We treat metabolic conditions such as type 2 diabetes, prediabetes, hypertension, and obesity as well as autoimmune and inflammatory conditions.

See how it works

A data-driven root-cause approach to medical care

Revero’s personalized nutrition therapy and clinical protocols are designed to help reduce blood sugar and inflammation. Also, Revero clinicians provide data-driven on-going remote medical support in a personalized based on each patients unique profile.

Learn about membership

“After many years working as an orthopedic surgeon I realized that despite the advances in healthcare, we were still failing to address disease in a way that truly made a difference. Revero was founded with a mission to turn this around by focusing on not just symptoms but treating the actual root causes of disease!”

Dr. Shawn Baker MD

CO-FOUNDER OF REVERO

Our Story

Dr. Shawn Baker MD is an orthopedic surgeon, international speaker, world champion athlete, and a leading authority on using nutrition therapy to treat chronic diseases.

Mahsa Rostami is an AI engineer in Silicon Valley, expert in building AI solutions for healthcare. She has developed AI models that improved healthcare services for millions of people.

In her personal journey with autoimmune symptoms Mahsa came across Dr. Shawn Baker’s protocol which helped her heal and feel amazing. They were inspired to create Revero together to make this revolutionary treatment accessible to everyone in a personalized way using the power of data science.

About us

Revero is a online medical clinic treating chronic health conditions with personalized medical treatments and precision nutrition. We treat metabolic conditions such as type 2 diabetes, prediabetes, primary hypertension, and obesity as well as autoimmune and inflammatory conditions.

Team

Mahsa Rostami

Founder & CEO

Mahsa is an AI engineer in Silicon Valley, expert in building AI solutions for healthcare. She has developed AI models that improved healthcare services for millions of people. Her personal health transformation inspired her to develop the Revero platform.

Dr. Shawn Baker MD

Founder & CMO

Shawn is an orthopedic surgeon, international speaker, best-selling author, and world champion athlete. He is a leading authority on low-carb nutritiona therapy and raising awareness about how it affects chronic diseases.

Dr. Elie Jarrouge MD

Lead Physician

Elie is a physician skilled in reversing obesity and diabetes through lifestyle changes, emphasizing the importance of addressing metabolic syndrome. He's committed to improving patient health through lifestyle interventions.

Dr. Adrian Blackwell MD

Medical Director

Adrian is a physician with vast experience, licensed in all 50 states, and committed to transforming healthcare into a cost-effective, accessible system. He is fueled by personal challenges as a patient and parent to chronically-ill children.

Dr. Jeff Curtis MD

Research Advisor, Rheumatology

Dr. Curtis, a Professor at UAB, specializes in Clinical Immunology and Rheumatology with degrees in Medicine, Public Health, Clinical Informatics, and Epidemiology. He is an expert in clinical research and has published hundreds of peer-reviewed papers.

Dr. Benjamin Bikman

Research & Scientific Advisor

Ben is a researcher on mechanisms of metabolic disorders, focusing on the roles of insulin and ketones as key drivers of metabolic function. He frequently published in peer-reviewed journals and presents at international science meetings.

Dr. Valerie Hoffman

Research & Scientific Advisor

Valerie is a leading researcher in digital health with over two decades of experience and has contributed to over 80 publications and studies, including clinical trials for digital health initiatives. She earned her MPH and a doctorate in psychiatric epidemiology.

Michael Paltiel, MD

Clinical Advisor, Dermatology

Michael is a board-certified dermatologist, graduate of University of Maryland Dermatology Residency, and Johns Hopkins School of Medicine. He has several research papers published in peer-reviewed dermatology journals.

*Due to state regulations, patients who have labs drawn in NJ, NY, and RI must self-pay for labs or contact their insurance provider for possible coverage.
