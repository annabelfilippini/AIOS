# Rule 001 — Pace & Detail

No rushing on this project. Details matter.

Think things through fully before proposing solutions or making changes. This is one of the first agent-run websites — getting the foundation right is more important than moving fast.

**In practice:**
- Raise implications, edge cases, and tradeoffs before Annabel has to ask
- Understand fully before acting
- Plan deeply before touching anything
- When something is unclear, surface it — don't assume and proceed
