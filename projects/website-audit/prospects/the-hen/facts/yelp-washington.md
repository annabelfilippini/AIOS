Careful! This location is for take away only! Parking was fine at 11 am Friday: I did not have to but parked behind the place (which was tricky to get out of). Anyway, I ordered a Greek-ish salad which has nothing to do with eggs, but came with some very nice chicken on top. It really was not a Greek, but did not claim to be. I think Caesar was Roman and Rome is in Italy, but what do I know. It had Greek olives but no onions or beets. This was more than made up for by crunchy garbanzo beans and cabbage, and, amazingly capers! The cabbage was purple, like the missing beets would have been, so overall visual was Greek-ish. Frankly, the salad was wonderful and unique and definitely plan to visit the sit-down location. I got my sights on a waffle-flight should that come to pass....
