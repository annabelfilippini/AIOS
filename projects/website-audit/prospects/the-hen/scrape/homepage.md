## FAQs

Find answers to common questions about our cafe, menu options, and locations.

What are your hours?

We’re open daily from 8 AM to 3 PM, with a well-deserved rest on Wednesdays. Stop by and enjoy our fresh offerings any other day!

Do you take reservations?

At this time, we operate on a first-come, first-served basis. Simply walk in, we'll take your order and you can pick any seat you'd like.

What's on your menu?

Our menu celebrates breakfast and brunch, with an array of thoughtfully crafted dishes—from fluffy pancakes and savory omelettes to a modern twist on avocado toast. You’ll find something delightful no matter your mood.

Do you have gluten-free options?

We have gluten-friendly choices that are sure to please. Feel free to browse our menu to find a dish that suits your needs.

Do you offer vegan options?

Absolutely. We’ve curated a selection of flavorful vegan dishes, so there’s always something for everyone.

Is outdoor seating available?

Yes, enjoy the sunshine with our outdoor seating area, perfect for savoring your meal in the open air.
