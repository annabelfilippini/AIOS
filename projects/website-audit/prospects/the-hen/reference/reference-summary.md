# Reference Sites — The Hen

Chosen 2026-04-20. Each ref was scraped to `reference/<brand>/`.

## Sadelle's — https://www.sadelles.com/
**Why chosen:** NYC brunch/bakery institution with global reach — peer-tier brunch identity, warmth over minimalism, and a chef-portrait homepage that anchors a multi-location brand in personality rather than locations grid.
**Execution takeaways for The Hen:**
- Lead the homepage with a single oversized portrait (chef or owner, not plated food) — builds identity before menu; The Hen's "Mom please send money" neon + people-first photos can play the same role.
- Location list is flat, text-only, uppercased — no map chrome, no cards. For The Hen's 3 Ann Arbor spots, a similarly restrained "ANN ARBOR · ON THE FLY · AT VALOR" bar beats a three-card grid and hides the asymmetry (dine-in vs carryout vs pop-up).
- Origin-story paragraph is short, emotionally specific ("dedicates to his great-grandmother, Sadelle"), and lives on the homepage — not buried under About. The Hen can do the same with its Jan 2025 rename story.
**Do NOT copy:** Sadelle's operates at a luxury/celebratory price tier (Bellagio, Paris, Riyadh); The Hen is casual Ann Arbor brunch — don't import the hotel-lobby formality or the global-locations swagger.

## Cutler & Co — https://cutlerandco.com.au/
**Why chosen:** Photo-forward editorial default (per feedback_editorial_photo_forward) — constrained hero, 8-image grid, underlined CTAs. Best-in-class pattern for a restaurant with strong visual assets and dense operational info (hours, location, contact) to deliver quietly.
**Execution takeaways for The Hen:**
- Hero is one constrained photo (not full-bleed); below it sits a compact info block (location / hours / contact) as three plain-text columns. The Hen's "hours vary by location" problem is easier to read in that grid than in cards.
- The 8-image grid (view-fullsize links, no captions) replaces a "gallery" page — photos do the selling. The Hen's pastry/interior/latte-art library is strong enough to carry this pattern.
- CTAs are underlined text links, not buttons ("Make a reservation"). For The Hen, that means SEE MENU / ORDER ONLINE / DIRECTIONS read as editorial links, not ecommerce buttons — keeps the café tone.
**Do NOT copy:** Cutler is a white-tablecloth reservation restaurant with a $240 rib eye — the pricing, tasting-menu formality, and evening-only hours belong to them. The Hen stays daytime, counter-service, walk-in.
