## SADELLE'S IS TRADITION

Founded in New York City in 2015 and now beloved around the world, Sadelle’s has reached iconic status thanks to its celebratory atmosphere, warm style of service, and a menu of classics that includes the best bagels in America, irresistible chopped salads, and famed specialties like Sadelle’s French Toast.

At the heart of Sadelle’s is a family legacy of food, flavor, and fun that Major Food Group co-founder Jeff Zalaznick dedicates to his great-grandmother, Sadelle. Her recipes were simply unforgettable, the menu at her namesake is a celebration of indulgence without pretense—decadent, comforting, and prepared with the highest levels of technique.
