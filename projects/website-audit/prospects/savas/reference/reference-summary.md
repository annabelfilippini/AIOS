# Reference Sites — Sava's
Chosen 2026-04-20. Each ref was scraped to `reference/<brand>/` (homepage + menu markdown + desktop-full screenshots). Sava's is the target: upscale-casual, globally-eclectic, Ann Arbor institution since 2007, bi-level dining room, Pulpo Group (Dixboro House, Aventura), warm palette (#DDBD6B gold + #A54223 rust).

## Wild Ginger — https://www.wildginger.net/
Why chosen: Institutional peer — "vibrant flavors of Asia since 1989," three locations, wine-led, family-style, long-running neighborhood fixture. The closest match for Sava's "dining institution since 2007" positioning with multi-location complexity (Pulpo's Dixboro + Aventura).
Execution takeaways for Sava's:
- Tagline carries the history as the hero line — "Honoring the vibrant flavors of Asia since 1989." Sava's equivalent is already written (Tock: "an Ann Arbor institution serving inventive local food… since 2007"); put it H1 instead of burying it under "Welcome to Ann Arbor's most spirited dining institution."
- Three-location reservation architecture cleanly labeled (Downtown / McKenzie / Bellevue) with one call-to-action per pill. Template for the Pulpo Group lockup — Sava's / Dixboro House / Aventura sit as a three-button cluster, not buried in a footer.
- Mission statement as editorial interlude between photo blocks — "Happiness, harmony, health, and the spice of life…" Lets warmth carry weight without turning into SaaS-copy. Sava's Tock line ("serving our community for lunch, brunch, and dinner seven days a week… Join us anytime, for any occasion") can run the same way.
- Food photography disciplined to honest plated shots (not styled-to-death editorial) — matches Sava's existing Squarespace photo library of bi-level dining room, brunch plates, cocktails.
- Wine-program callout as its own section ("Stop by and have a glass"). Sava's happy-hour (Mon–Fri 3–6 PM) deserves the same dedicated module instead of living only in the Google knowledge panel.
Do NOT copy: The Southeast-Asian color palette (turquoise + rust accent) or the "Joy of Living is the Joy of Giving" gift-card voice — that's Rick and Ann Yoder's 35-year-old identity. Sava's gold-and-rust palette is its own; the execution discipline transfers, the chrome does not.

## Sarma — https://www.sarmarestaurant.com/
Why chosen: Globally-eclectic menu that refuses to pick a country (Turkish meyhane frame, Middle Eastern meze, Japanese eggplant, Tunisian brik, Mexican al pastor) — the closest editorial peer for Sava's American-meets-Mediterranean-meets-Asian menu (Bang Bang Shrimp next to Greek Lamb Burger next to Capellini Marlon). Plus James Beard-nominated chef lineage (Cassie Piuma, Ana Sortun) shows how to put a team forward without turning it into a biography dump.
Execution takeaways for Sava's:
- Menu rendered inline as dense typed prose — dish name bold, ingredient list lowercase, price at end (`BUCKWHEAT SIMIT whipped tahini butter, apricot jam 5`). Zero photos on the menu page. Directly solves Sava's Squarespace-menu problem without a CMS migration. For Sava's this lets "Greek Lamb Burger · house ground lamb, feta, salt roasted beet, pepperoncini, pickled red onion 26" sit as its own line item instead of hiding behind a Yelp click-through.
- Menu grouped by small evocative section headers (`EKMEK`, `SHMEAR`, `MEYHANE SNACKS`, `BALIK`, `SHISH`) instead of generic "Starters/Entrees." Sava's could do the same with its own voice — "Brunch Always," "Bar Bites," "Share," "The Burger Program."
- Team page = three portraits + role + one paragraph each (Cassie, Liz, Ana). No corporate bios, just why-they-cook. Sava's Pulpo Group leadership gets the same treatment if the owner/chef is named — and per `feedback_slow_down_details` this is worth walking through with Annabel before writing copy from whole cloth.
- "Our Partners" strip at the bottom links sister restaurants (Oleana, Sofra, Siena Farms) as logo tiles. This is the pattern for Sava's → Dixboro House + Aventura cross-linking, already surfaced under "Partners" on Sava's Tock page.
- Hours + reservations + parking + 20%-for-6 policy written in plain declarative English ("Reservations are available online up to 30 days in advance…"). No legal hedge. Sava's Graduation Weekend deposit ($25/pp) and 1–15 party cap belong in the same voice, not hidden on Tock.
Do NOT copy: The teal/mustard Turkish meze palette, the `EKMEK`/`BALIK` section labels themselves, or the 20-seat-bar-only identity. Sava's is bi-level, full-service, 1–15-top reservations — not a meze bar. Borrow the menu typography and team-page restraint, not Sarma's specific cultural posture.

## King — https://kingrestaurant.nyc/
Why chosen: The canonical "neighborhood restaurant + celebratory destination" positioning — literally in their About copy. Sava's is the same job for Ann Arbor (U-M graduation dinners, Best Romantic Dinner 2019, plus a $20-30/person neighborhood happy hour). King also leans on a NYT press quote ("Pete Wells in his 2018 review… how many little moves they know that can raise a recipe from good to exceptional") — direct template for how Sava's should deploy its *Michigan Daily* quote.
Execution takeaways for Sava's:
- About paragraph reads as one continuous sentence of restraint — "relaxed, simple and elegant… muted tones and white tablecloths to the ever-changing offerings… it is both a neighborhood restaurant as well as a celebratory destination." Sava's can copy the dual-identity sentence structure (not the words) — neighborhood brunch AND graduation-weekend dinner, same room.
- Press quote set as body copy in the About paragraph, not pulled out as a garish testimonial tile. Sava's *Michigan Daily* "Best Romantic Dinner… classiest and most impressive first date, fifth date or who-knows-what date" belongs in that exact slot, attributed inline.
- Hours section labels dayparts by name — `DINNER`, `LUNCH`, `CAFÉ KING`. Sava's runs brunch + happy hour + dinner out of one kitchen; a three-daypart layout (`BRUNCH` 9 AM–3 PM / `HAPPY HOUR` 3–6 PM / `DINNER` 6 PM–10/11 PM) would finally make the daypart structure legible. Tock already shows the 3 PM/4 PM split on today's page — surface it.
- Photography is dining-room-forward, not plate-forward: white tablecloths, sconces, the bar room. Sava's bi-level interior (cited as "notably has two levels" in Yelp reviews, "elegant" in Ned I.'s 6/27/25 review) is unique and underused — lead with the room, not the Bang Bang Shrimp.
- Cookbook callout at the top and bottom (`The King Cookbook is now available`). Sava's doesn't have a cookbook, but Pulpo Group's multi-property announcement or a seasonal-menu drop fits the same slot.
Do NOT copy: King's white-tablecloth Southern-France/Italy identity or the ultra-restrained monochrome palette. Sava's is warm (gold + rust) and spirited; the words used by the restaurant are "most spirited" and "celebrate" — not "relaxed" and "muted." Borrow the dual-identity framing and press-quote placement; the posture stays Sava's.

## La Semilla — https://www.lasemilla.kitchen/
Why chosen: Modern Latin, Atlanta's Reynoldstown — a younger/spicier peer that demonstrates how to render a warm palette (terracotta + pine green + cream) without defaulting to generic-restaurant-beige. Also a flagship concept of a parent group (Happy Seed), matching Sava's Pulpo Group structure. And its menu is shown as six image tiles (`1.png` through `6.png`) — a cautionary tale for the Do-Not-Copy bullet.
Execution takeaways for Sava's:
- Wordmark/submark system with consistent pine-green glyph reused across hero, menu header, map marker, footer. Sava's has a visible brand lockup on Facebook/Instagram — the redesign should treat it as an architectural element (edge of page, footer signature, map marker) the way La Semilla does, not just slap it at the top.
- Editorial Spanish-English bilingual voice ("sólo plantas," "sit back, relax, & enjoy the possibilities,,,") gives the brand a signature. Sava's has its own signature phrase already — "most spirited dining institution" — that should appear more than once on the homepage, not just as an H1.
- Parent-group lockup sits as its own line: "La Semilla is the flagship concept by Happy Seed, grown from Atlanta, Georgia." Sava's gets: "Sava's is the founding restaurant of Pulpo Group — Ann Arbor." Directly answers the branding puzzle the PulpoGroup_04.jpg hero file half-hints at today.
- Live Instagram grid pulled in at the bottom (four recent posts with captions) keeps the page fresh without adding CMS load. Sava's @savas_ann_arbor has 7.4K+ FB followers and an active IG — same move applies.
- Hero treatment = signature dish on a colored ground, not a dining-room shot. Works when the dish is iconic (chochoyotes, elote en vaso). Sava's equivalent: Bang Bang Shrimp (93 Yelp reviews, 48 photos — most-photographed dish). One dish, one saturated warm ground, one tagline.
Do NOT copy: The six-image-PNG menu implementation — it's the exact problem Sarma solves with inline HTML, and La Semilla's version is *less* accessible than what Sava's already has. Copy La Semilla's identity discipline, inherit Sarma's menu rendering. Also do not copy the terracotta/pine palette itself or the bilingual Spanish voice — Sava's palette is gold-and-rust and its voice is Ann Arbor English.

## Guiding principle
References inform execution quality: the tagline-as-hero (Wild Ginger), inline-HTML menu with evocative section headers (Sarma), dual-identity neighborhood-plus-celebratory About paragraph with press quote inline (King), parent-group lockup + signature-dish hero + live IG grid (La Semilla). These are mechanics — not identity.

Sava's identity stays Sava's own: Ann Arbor's spirited dining institution since 2007, bi-level dining room at 216 S State St, the Pulpo Group family with Dixboro House and Aventura, the warm gold/rust palette (#DDBD6B + #A54223), the American-meets-global menu that refuses to pick a lane, the 3–6 PM happy hour, the Sunday brunch buffet, the graduation-weekend rhythm tied to the University of Michigan. Per `feedback_elevate_not_erase` — elevate the execution, don't overwrite the identity. Reference sites are ingredients, not templates.
