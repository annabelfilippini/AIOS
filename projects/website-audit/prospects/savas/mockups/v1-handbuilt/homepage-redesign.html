The Room

A bi-level dining room on State Street.

Contemporary, warm, and unmistakably Ann Arbor — a downtown room across from the University that guests often call elegant, and notably, has two levels. Upstairs for a quieter seat; downstairs for the bar and the buzz.

The private dining room books for birthdays, rehearsals, graduations, and any reason to bring a group.
