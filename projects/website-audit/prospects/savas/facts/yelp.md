I came here on a Sunday morning with my friend around 10:30 and the ambience was really great. I love the way that they set up the interior decor. I will say that the first level is a lot more elegant, compared to the second level. I don't really like how the first level is reserve reserved for boots for large parties of people because my friend and I were seated on the upper level and the ambience is not as great as it was below.



Now, in terms of the food, I will say that the food was delicious. The food was a little bit cold when I came out to us but I wasn't sure if it was supposed to be that way. We ordered the egg white omelette and I had a really great combination platter of tomatoes, cheddar, and white eggs. We also ordered the bang bang shrimp, which we honestly prefer preferred compared to the eggs because the shrimp was very flavorful and the crispy rice on the bottom was to die for. The food was also very filling.



In terms of service, they have college students that are servers. Our server was all right. I wouldn't say that he went above and beyond. We ask for hot chili sauce with our food and the waiter brought out normal hot sauce, so I'm sure there was a misunderstanding there in terms of going above and beyond. But it wasn't bad.



All in all, I would give it a nine out of 10 for the food, 10 out of 10 for the ambience and the Service 7/10.

The combination of the beautiful ambiance and the high quality food make Sava's a desirable destination.
