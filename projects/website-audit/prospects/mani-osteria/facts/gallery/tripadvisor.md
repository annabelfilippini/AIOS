### [Pretty good food, but very awkward](https://www.tripadvisor.com/ShowUserReviews-g29556-d2196441-r1038080294-Mani_Osteria-Ann_Arbor_Michigan.html)

Nov 2025 • Couples

We arrived at about 4:15pm and the restaurant was completely empty. We came in on the Division side of the restaurant and walked up to the bar where the hostess and another employee were chatting. They pretty much ignored us so I asked if we seated ourselves or did we need to be seated.. We were told the hostess would seat us but we needed to go over to the other door. Huh? Why do they have a door on Division if you can only be seated if you enter from the door on Liberty? So, we went back to the other door and started over. The hostess seemed quite flustered as she scanned what I assume was the seating diagram on the computer. She asked us to stay at the front door while she walked around the seating area checking something out. Huh? The restaurant was completely empty, was it possible to squeeze us in somewhere? I could swear it felt like a SNL comedy routine except that she was serious. So, we asked for a table by the window and she said that that section was closed. Why would part of the restaurant be open and part (the best part where most people want to sit), be closed? So, we sat at a table on the other side of the restaurant as required. Shortly after we were seated another group came in and were given the same song and dance. They had also asked for a window table but obeyed the hostess' need to sit them away from the window. Apparently, they got fed up and demanded that they be seated by the window and the hostess promptly moved them to the table that we had asked for. So, when our waiter came to our table, I asked what was going on. He told us that the manager uses that section of the restaurant as his office and guests cannot use the tables by the window while he is doing business. It reminded me that when we owned a deli that no owner, manager or employee was allowed to park in front of the store. We parked at the back of the lot and left the good spots for our customers. Apparently, management at Mani's views customers not as guests but as a necessary inconvenience. Oddly, Mani does have a private dining room that was vacant but, I guess if you are the manager, you get the best section in the house to use as your office. The food and service were generally good though we had to send the meatballs back because they were served at room temperature not hot. The second batch did come out hot. But, not feeling too welcome here, I don't think we'll be back.
