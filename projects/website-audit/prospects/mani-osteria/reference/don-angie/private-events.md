# Private Dining at Don Angie

![](https://images.getbento.com/accounts/d704d1f12e9025a5303f862eeb87f5c0/media/images/16578Don-s-Next-Door_Dining-Room_006.jpg?w=1200&fit=crop&auto=compress,format&cs=origin&crop=focalpoint&fp-x=0.44&fp-y=0.99)

Don Angie is available for private events and buyouts. Don's Next Door -- our new, dedicated private dining room next to the restaurant with its own entrance, cocktail area, and kitchen -- is available for seated dinners of up to 18 guests. For larger groups, we offer full restaurant buyouts accommodating up to 50 guests. For more information on arranging a special event at Don Angie, please contact Matt Crane at [mcrane@qualitybranded.com](mailto:mcrane@qualitybranded.com) or fill out the form below and we will get back to you!
