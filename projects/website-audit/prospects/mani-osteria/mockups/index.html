Welcome

A family-friendly osteria on East Liberty, serving wood-fired pizzas, handmade pastas, and signature antipasti since 2011.

Mani is our neighborhood osteria — a place built for lingering. The oven runs all night, the kitchen is open to the room, and the menu turns with what's good that week.

Blistered crusts. House-made pasta. Antipasti meant to share. And a bar program that takes cocktails as seriously as the food — Aperol spritzes on happy hour, amaros late.

Walk in for a bite at the bar. Book the whole dining room for a celebration. Whatever the shape, we keep it warm, loud, and generous — the way an osteria should be.
