Food was honestly phenomenal. We LOVED the carne asada plate. The steak was so tender for being so thin (not easy to accomplish) and had great flavors. The mole on the cheese enchilada was delicious as well. And I'm a sucker for a cheese enchilada and believe it or not, it can be messed up. But not here!



The service was solid. No complaints.



The chairs are SUPER comfortable. Definitely not typical. Nice decor. Trendy/classy vibes. It's pretty big as well. Easy do a big group around noon but we did come once on a weekend evening and it was too packed to even get in so I'm sure it varies!



Overall I'm impressed. I'll definitely be back!

New place, who dis?!?



Ya gotta love Yelp. We never eat poorly (ok, it's \*really\* rare) and we also find new places local to us.



Where else ya gonna get that kinda love? Just Yelp. Obvi, I'm a fan, lol.



Onto the review - Adelitas is a great example of hidden gems (for us). Edgewater isn't far from us, but it's not close enough where we'd know about all the new places. If ya know me, you already know we sat at the bar. Yep, we did. And that's an easy way to learn about some of the cool history of a place - like we did.



We shared their queso fundido - loved the cheesy gooeyness. He had their carne asada plate (charred a little too much but no other complaints) and I enjoyed their chicken enchiladas with their verde sauce (def a good choice).



The portions are large as the pics show. Leftovers FTW. Pricing was appropriate, nothing that threw me. I'd def return for their spicy marg.



Their bartender was great - chatty but not too over attentive and really friendly. He shared the place's history. Cheers to women owned businesses - this one is also family owned and founded by a Latina chef, Silvia Andaya. We need more passion projects.



Thanks, Silvia and team. We're excited to have another great option for south of the border fare!
