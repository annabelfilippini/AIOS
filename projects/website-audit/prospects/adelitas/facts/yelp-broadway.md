A friend recommended this spot, and I'm so glad!

We went on taco Tuesday and had no problem getting bar seating (or there were a few tables available) around 5pm but I noticed after 6pm people were waiting for seating. The food was delicious! We got $2 chips and salsa off the happy hour menu and the queso con soyrizo (not on HH menu) which was served with warm tortillas. Honestly we probably didn't need all that because the tacos were so good I could've just filled up on more of the tacos, but still was a great meal! I ordered the lengua, pastor, and taco special (breaded shrimp). They were all so good. Think taco quality of a good street taco spot but with the ambiance of a cute tex mex bar. The bartender was great and made a solid marg ($8 on happy hour).

You get a great deal on taco Tuesday but I'm sure you'd get great service and quality any day of the week!

Went to Adelitas with friends and it was pretty good! My friend and I did the mix-and-match tacos, and our other friend got a burrito. Everything was super flavorful and we all enjoyed our meals. The drinks were good too!



The chips came with four different house sauces and they were all delicious (I wish I remembered the names ). I'd definitely order those again. I also got the queso .. I wasn't expecting it to taste more like shredded cheese, but that might've been on me lol.



Parking was easy and free on the street, which is always a plus. My only downside is that I wish the restaurant was bigger. It started to feel a little squished as more people came in. Overall though, service was quick and the staff were really friendly. I'd come back!
