№ 02 The Woman Behind the Name A Feature
Chapter One · Michoacán

Chef Silvia
Andaya.

Owner, executive chef, and matriarch of three Denver restaurants.

Adelitas is named for La Adelita — the soldadera, the woman-warrior of the Mexican Revolution. She cooked, she healed the wounded, and she fought. She is, Silvia would tell you, every Mexican mother.

Silvia Andaya's cooking is rooted in Michoacán — the region Mexicans call 'the soul of Mexico.' Mole from her family's kitchen. Carnitas the way her grandmother cooked them. Tender birria. Oaxacan cheese. Recipes that traveled from her mother's table to the Denver line every night.

That's the restaurant. A tribute to the unsung heroines. A family room in Denver that eats like Michoacán. Bienvenidos.

The Meaning of the Name

¿Quién fue La Adelita?

A soldadera of the Mexican Revolution. She followed her sergeant, prepared the meals, mended the clothing, cared for the wounded, and fought in the battles. Over time 'Adelita' became the name for every woman who carried the war on her back. It means bravery. Altruism. Strength. It's the name on our door for a reason.

№ 03 Michoacán, on a plate. Cooked Tuesday through Sunday

Recipes from Silvia's family kitchen, cooked every night on Broadway and in Edgewater.

№ 01

Molcajete de Adelitas

Marinated steak, chicken, and shrimp in a bold tomato sauce. Nopales, green onions, panela, melted cheddar. It comes out sizzling.

№ 02

Al Pastor Tacos

Marinated pork on the trompo, pineapple, cilantro, onion. Three to an order, corn or flour.

№ 03

Chile Rellenos

Two poblanos stuffed with white cheddar, battered, fried, smothered. Refried beans on the side.

№ 04

Chicken Mole

Silvia's family mole. Toasted chilies, Mexican chocolate, a long afternoon. Served over chicken with rice and beans.

№ 05

Carne Asada Tacos

Charred steak, white onion, cilantro, a wedge of lime. Three to an order, on warm corn tortillas.

№ 06

Street Tacos

The Tuesday-night plate. Small tortillas, a hit of salsa, the way you eat them in Michoacán.

№ 07

Chips & Salsa Trio

Three salsas — roja, verde, and the smoky one from morita chiles. Warm chips on the table.

№ 08

Queso Fundido

Bubbling Oaxaca cheese, chorizo or rajas. Tortillas to fold around it, while it's hot.
