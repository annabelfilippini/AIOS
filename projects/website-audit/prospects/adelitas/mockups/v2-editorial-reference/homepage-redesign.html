№ 02 The Woman Behind the Name A Feature
Chapter One · Michoacán

Mole from her
mother's kitchen,
cooked every night.

Adelitas is named for La Adelita — the soldadera, the woman-warrior of the Mexican Revolution. She cooked, she healed the wounded, and she fought. She is, Silvia would tell you, every Mexican mother.

Silvia Andaya's cooking is rooted in Michoacán — the region Mexicans call el alma de México, the soul of Mexico. Mole from her family's kitchen. Carnitas the way her grandmother cooked them. Tender birria. Oaxacan cheese. Recipes that traveled from her mother's table to the Denver line every night.

She runs three rooms now — Adelitas on Broadway, Adelitas in Edgewater, and La Doña Mezcaleria, the small mezcal-forward bar tucked in behind the kitchen. The food is the same kitchen. The hand is the same hand.

What you sit down to here is not Mexican food translated for somewhere else. It is Michoacán — moved, but not changed. Bienvenidos.

Think taco quality of a good street taco spot but with the ambiance of a cute tex-mex bar. — Sarah W. · Yelp · Broadway · Feb 2026

№ 01

Carne Asada Tacos

Charred steak, white onion, cilantro, a wedge of lime. Three to an order, on warm corn tortillas.

№ 02

Al Pastor Tacos

Marinated pork from the trompo, cilantro, onion. House family recipe, eaten by the dozen.

№ 03

Chile Rellenos

Two poblanos stuffed with white cheddar, battered, fried, smothered. Refried beans alongside.

№ 04

Chicken Mole

Silvia's family mole. Toasted chilies, Mexican chocolate, a long afternoon. Over chicken, with rice and beans.

№ 05

Molcajete de Adelitas

Steak, chicken and shrimp in a bold tomato sauce. Nopales, panela, melted cheese. Out of the kitchen sizzling.

№ 06

Street Tacos

The Tuesday-night plate. Small tortillas, a hit of salsa, the way you eat them in Michoacán.

№ 07

Chips & Salsa Trio

Three salsas — roja, verde, and the smoky one Silvia makes from morita chiles. Warm chips on the table.

№ 08

Queso Fundido

Bubbling Oaxaca cheese, chorizo or rajas. Tortillas to fold around it, while it's hot.

№ 04 The Bar Program Tequila · Mezcal · La Doña
A Deep Agave Program

A long pour list,
and a mezcaleria
behind the kitchen.

Adelitas runs one of Denver's deepest agave programs — Blancos, Reposados, Añejos, single-estate bottles you won't find on most lists. Step behind the Broadway kitchen and you'll find La Doña, Silvia's mezcal-forward bar, open later, small enough that the bartender knows what you drink.
