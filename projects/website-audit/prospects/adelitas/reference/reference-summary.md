# Reference Sites — Adelitas Cocina y Cantina
Chosen 2026-04-14. Scraped to `reference/{atomix,cosme,pujol}/` during original run; scrape-bodies pruned by `/audit-cleanup` after ship (Apr 15). Breadcrumb retrofitted 2026-04-16 from `scrape-data.md` lines 162–184.

## Atomix — https://atomixnyc.com
Why chosen: Korean tasting menu, Junghyun Park. Editorial, typography-driven chef-as-protagonist pattern. Aspirational-peer for "what world-class chef storytelling looks like on the web."
Execution takeaways for Adelitas:
- Full-bleed hero behaves like a magazine cover — extreme negative space, serif display type over monochrome food photography.
- Chef is the protagonist, not the menu. The Silvia Andaya + "La Adelita" heritage story has this tier of narrative potential and currently lives buried inside the `/tequilas-family-mexican-restaurant` SEO doorway.
- Confidence through restraint: near-zero chrome, no decorative flourishes, typography carries the emotion.
Do NOT copy: Atomix's monochrome/fine-dining palette. Adelitas is a warm neighborhood cantina — warmth and Mexicanidad must survive.

## Cosme — https://cosmenyc.com
Why chosen: Enrique Olvera's NYC flagship. Mexican fine-dining. Proves an editorial Mexican restaurant site doesn't need sombreros, agave clichés, or loud color to signal identity.
Execution takeaways for Adelitas:
- Menu page renders as a simple branded list — permission to let the food names carry the copy instead of florid descriptions.
- Deep tonal neutrals + confident typography (serif display + sans body). Signals premium-but-Mexican without kitsch.
- Text-rich layout (more than Atomix) — a working template for a restaurant that wants to communicate narrative, not just atmosphere.
Do NOT copy: Cosme's quiet/premium posture. Adelitas is "lively and welcoming" per Yelp — the energy in its room must come through in the mockup. Borrow the restraint; keep the warmth.

## Pujol — https://pujol.com.mx/en
Why chosen: Enrique Olvera's Mexico City flagship. Global benchmark for modern Mexican dining. Reference for the ceiling, not a template.
Execution takeaways for Adelitas:
- Extremely minimal homepage: single hero, nav, reservation CTA. Everything else is deferred. A counterpoint to Adelitas's current Showit build which spreads thin across doorway pages.
- Reservation is the primary CTA above the fold — direct-booking logic, not "see menu / find location / subscribe / shop / ..." menu-sprawl.
- JS-heavy site where the screenshot carries more brand than the markdown does — design is load-bearing.
Do NOT copy: Pujol-level minimalism. Adelitas is a neighborhood cantina with two locations and active programming — it needs more surface area than Pujol. Take the above-fold discipline, not the austerity.

## Guiding principle (carry forward to any future re-run)
References inform *execution quality* (typography, photography hierarchy, restraint, chef-as-protagonist narrative structure).
Identity stays Adelitas's own: warmth, family, Michoacán, La Adelita woman-warrior heritage, two-location Denver neighborhood presence. Per `feedback_elevate_not_erase` — elevate, don't erase.
