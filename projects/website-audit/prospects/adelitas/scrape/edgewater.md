Our Story

“La Adelita” is the story of a young woman during the Mexican Revolution who falls in love with a sergeant, leaves her humble home behind, and travels with him and his regiment. “La Adelita” became an archetype of a woman warrior during this era. An Adelita was a soldadera, or woman soldier, who took on various supporting roles to her beloved. She prepared meals, mended clothing, cared for the wounded, and even fought in battles against Mexican government forces. With the passage of time, the word “Adelita” came to be used for all the soldaderas, marking their place in history as a vital force in the Mexican revolutionary war efforts. The term “La Adelita” symbolizes a brave, altruistic woman with an abundance of strength and courage.

"La Adelita"

Adelitas Cocina y Cantina brings traditional Mexican fare and Agave cocktails to your Platte Park neighborhood. We are a Latin and Woman-owned and operated family restaurant. We have made a collaborative effort to bring you a relaxing, comfortable, safe, and enjoyable atmosphere, serving traditional Mexican food from the state of Michoacán, Mexico. We offer lunch and dinner 6 days a week and a late-night menu to satisfy your after dark cravings. We are a family-oriented restaurant but after 9:00 pm, we dim the lights and provide you with a warm and relaxing atmosphere.

We chose the Adelita name to pay tribute to our mothers. They have inspired us to share with you our traditional family recipes and refreshing cocktails in a relaxing and safe environment.

Adelitas Cocina y Cantina

Adelitas Cocina Y Cantina is a passion project founded by matriarch owner and head chef, Silvia Andaya. Featuring the premiere selection of mezcal available to Colorado, a deep commitment to traditional Oaxacan fare and craft, agave forward cocktails, Adelitas Cocina Y Cantina brings to you an authentic oasis from the traditions south of the border.
