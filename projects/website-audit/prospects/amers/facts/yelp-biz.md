A lot of seating, could accommodate small or large groups. Street parking in this area sucks and the garage is around the block.



Overall... I wasn't happy with the price $$ or too blown-away by the size or taste of my sandwich or dog but I want to love this deli because of the service and prime location. I would probably return to try another sandwich and hopefully bump up my rating from 3 to 5 stars.

I have now been a couple timed to amer's and have taken other people as it is reliable and pretty good. I've had the tuna salad sandwich, tomato bagel, lox bagel, and tried the eggs...I wouldn't get the bagel (I think revive has a better one in the are) this one is really bready and thick...being from NY I am not a huge fan. The sandwich however was very good and it is served with the crunchiest pickles. The eggs are also very good, definitely would recommend breakfast options; I want to try the french toast. My dad tried the hoagie with peppers and beef, not a huge fan. Stick to breakfast and pastrami/classics.



There tends to be a little bit of a line if you go around lunch time but it moves quick and is worth it. Reasonably priced and convenient. There is also ample seating and it's a goof place to get work done if you like noise around you.
