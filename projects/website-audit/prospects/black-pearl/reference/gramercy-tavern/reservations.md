# Reservations

We accept reservations 28 days in advance, new dates become available at noon. Please call us from 12pm to 9pm daily at [212-477-0777 Phone number](tel:2124770777) or make an online reservation below.

## The Dining Room

![](https://images.getbento.com/accounts/c96fa153fcd99197529b15a71ab913e4/media/images/728772025-01-14-USHG-ReadMcKendree-0254_V2.jpg?w=1000&fit=max&auto=compress,format&cs=origin&h=1000)

Our spacious Dining Room is one of New York's most acclaimed settings for seasonally focused cuisine.

For dinner service, we showcase a five-course seasonal menu and optional wine pairings. The ever-evolving menu highlights local greenmarket produce through Chef Michael Anthony’s contemporary cooking. Please refer to our menu page for more details.

Our lunchis a customizable dining experience made for busy meeting days and leisurely afternoons alike. Guests can choose between our à la carte menu or optional four-course menu.

Dietary restrictions can be accommodated.

## The Tavern

![](https://images.getbento.com/accounts/c96fa153fcd99197529b15a71ab913e4/media/images/133052025-01-14-USHG-ReadMcKendree-0123_V1.jpg?w=1000&fit=max&auto=compress,format&cs=origin&h=1000)

The Tavern serves an à la carte menu of soulful fare from the open, wood-burning grill in a lively, casual setting.

Our kitchen is open throughout the day and we accept reservations for table seating.  The bar is available for walk-ins at all times.
