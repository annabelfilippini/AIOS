# Adam Matthews — Drive-Share Companion Note

## The Message

> Hi there, I just sent you an email with a video and figured I'd drop it here as well, in case it's easier to watch 🙂
>
> Pepper Pong.mp4

Delivered via standard Google Drive share notification. No separate pitch email observed — this sentence is the entire note.

## Why It Works

- **Opens with what he did**, not who he is. No "I'm Adam, founder of…" preamble.
- **Acknowledges the recipient has options** — "in case it's easier to watch." Low-pressure, assumes nothing.
- **Single emoji softens the cold contact.** Reads human, not corporate.
- **Zero CTA.** The video is the CTA. The note just delivers it.
- **No signature block.** Just the sentence.
- **Parallel-channel framing** ("also dropping it here") is useful — tells the recipient there's another copy in email, so they can pick their channel.

## What to Steal

- **The "just in case" framing** — gives the recipient an out. Opposite of "please watch this."
- **One sentence is enough.** If the video does the work, the companion note only needs to point at it.
- **A single emoji at the end** is fine and warms the tone without being unprofessional.

## What NOT to Steal

- **He's using this as the primary send** — Annabel's pipeline uses the Drive share *plus* an email companion. Adam's note is the model for the Drive share text, not necessarily the email body.
- **No personalization beyond "Hi there."** Annabel's version uses first names — she has them from the sheet.
