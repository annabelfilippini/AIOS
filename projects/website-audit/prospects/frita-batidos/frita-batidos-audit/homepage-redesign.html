The Story

Cuban soul,
Michigan roots.

Founded by chef Eve Aronoff in 2010, Frita Batidos brings the Cuban street food tradition to downtown Ann Arbor. Inspired by the frita cubana sold at Havana lunch counters, every burger is built on locally sourced ingredients and a Slow Food philosophy.

We strive to source our meat, cheese and produce from Michigan or the Midwest whenever possible. All chicken and beef is certified Halal.
