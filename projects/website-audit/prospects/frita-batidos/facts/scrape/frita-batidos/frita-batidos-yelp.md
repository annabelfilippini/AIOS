First time eating here. Went after a movie on a Sunday. You eat at shared picnic tables, ordering your food at the counter. It's then delivered to you. We tried a chicken Frita, the chorizo, Frita, and one of the beef Fritas. They all come with fries on top. We also added the tropical slaw. Very fresh and tasty food. The service was friendly and quick. Great casual eatery. Give some Cuban street food a try!
