"# Rare Bird Rooftop — takeaways\n\n**Layout pattern:** Full-bleed hero image with centered overlay headline (serif display) + subhead + single CTA. After hero, generous whitespace, single editorial photo, then 3-column info strip (LOCATION / HOURS / MENU) — each labeled with tiny caps eyebrow. Very airy, lots of empty vertical space as \"luxury\" cue. Squarespace grid, no hard max-width.\n\n**Typography:** Display serif (freight-display-pro 120px / line-height 100 — very tight) over sans body (europa). H1 is lowercase, almost whispered. H3 eyebrows are 10px, 4px letter-spacing, uppercase — classic hospitality tic. Headlines live at ~120px on desktop; body at 16px.\n\n**Motion cues:** Scroll indicator icon in hero (\"Scroll\" + caret), no flashy reveals. Hero image is parallax-capable (data-parallax attr). Hover states are subtle underline/color — restrained.\n\n**Social proof:** None on main page. No press logos, no reviews. Relies entirely on photography to sell the vibe.\n\n**STEAL for Frita:** The 3-column \"LOCATION / HOURS / MENU\" strip with tiny uppercase eyebrows + centered serif values. It's the clearest way to answer visitor's practical questions without cluttering the hero.\n\n**AVOID for Frita:** Whispered lowercase serif headlines + no social proof. Frita's identity is loud, playful, award-winning — going quiet would erase their edge. Keep the shouting caps, keep the awards visible.\n"
