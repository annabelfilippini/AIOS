"# King Restaurant NYC — takeaways\n\n**Layout pattern:** Extreme narrow single-column (everything centered, ~400px content width even on desktop). Hand-drawn script logo + address at top. Alternating text blocks and square-crop photos stacked vertically. No hero image at all — just top banner bar (\"WE ARE THRILLED...\"). Feels like a printed menu or letter. Massive section padding (58px top/bottom).\n\n**Typography:** Adobe Garamond Pro for EVERYTHING — body, headings, buttons. 14px body, 20px headings with 4.6px letter-spacing tracked wide, Title Case not ALL-CAPS. Hand-drawn script for the logo only. One font family, heavy reliance on letter-spacing to create hierarchy instead of size.\n\n**Motion cues:** None detectable. Static site. No autoplay, no reveals, no parallax. Restraint as statement — \"we are serious, the food is the show.\"\n\n**Social proof:** Pete Wells 2018 NYT review quote inline in the About paragraph. Press link in footer. No logo strip — just one pull quote, one time, from the right critic.\n\n**STEAL for Frita:** The single-critic pull quote woven into body copy. More credible than a logo strip. Frita has Michigan Daily / Metro Times / Hour Magazine — pull one punchy line per reviewer.\n\n**AVOID for Frita:** The ultra-narrow single-column, zero-motion, monochrome-Garamond austerity. King sells hushed fine dining; Frita sells beach-party burgers. This layout would kill Frita's energy instantly.\n"
