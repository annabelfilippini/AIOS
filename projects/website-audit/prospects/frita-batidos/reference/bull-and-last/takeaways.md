"# The Bull & Last — takeaways\n\n**Layout pattern:** Full-bleed hero photo with centered uppercase serif headline + sparkle divider. Below, rhythm of wide image-left / text-right blocks alternating with centered text sections. Rooms section uses a horizontal carousel (arrow navigation). Shop uses 3-up image card row. Bottom has a full-width quote/CTA card with beige tile background. Grid feels editorial, not gridded — content floats in whitespace.\n\n**Typography:** Joanna Nova serif for ALL headings at 300–600 weight, uppercase, tight negative letter-spacing (-2.4px on H1 96px). Neue Montreal sans for body (16px). Every heading uppercase serif, tracked tight — unified voice across every section.\n\n**Motion cues:** Horizontal Rooms carousel with arrow controls. Subtle hover on product cards. No autoplay video. Scroll-triggered reveals appear minimal (they're selling craft, not motion).\n\n**Social proof:** Thin on home. Instagram CTA card (\"Follow us on Instagram\") as a beige block near footer. Careers/jobs copy block signals prestige (\"talented individuals who aspire to better\"). No press strip, no review quotes.\n\n**STEAL for Frita:** The unified heading treatment — one font, always uppercase, always tight-tracked. Creates a confident brand voice without much work. Frita could apply this to its own display font.\n\n**AVOID for Frita:** The sparse hospitality restraint (tiny buttons, beige-on-beige, Instagram CTA as only social proof). Frita needs to SHOUT its awards and multi-city footprint — this site whispers, Frita brags.\n"
