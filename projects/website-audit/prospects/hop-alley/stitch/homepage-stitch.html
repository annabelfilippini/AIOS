DENVER'S CHINATOWN. ORIGINAL. TORN DOWN.

SIGNATURE — Existing copy elevated

In the late 19th century, this area was the heart of Denver's vibrant Chinatown. Wazee Street was a corridor of community and commerce until a 1880 riot led to its destruction.

Hop Alley was the colloquial name given to this neighborhood. We chose this name to honor the history of the site we inhabit. Our kitchen is built on these bones, continuing a legacy of Chinese culinary tradition in the heart of RiNo.

TOMMY LEE

Restaurateur Tommy Lee opened Hop Alley in 2015 as a follow-up to his ramen powerhouse, Uncle. Lee's vision was to create a modern Chinese restaurant that captured the spirit of Denver's RiNo neighborhood while respecting the traditional roots of Sichuan cooking.
