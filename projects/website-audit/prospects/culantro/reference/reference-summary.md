# Reference Sites — Culantro

Chosen 2026-04-21. Each ref was scraped to `reference/<brand>/` (screenshots + markdown).

Annabel's direction: **Cutler & Co as editorial anchor, BUT more colorful and warmer** — Culantro's gold/ochre/flame-orange palette on a rich dark (or warm cream) ground, not Cutler's stark black-on-cream. Adopt Mission Ceviche's two-location picker pattern (she specifically called this out). Pull from Gjelina for multi-location IA. Avoid templating — customize around Culantro's mural-rich, owner-operator DNA.

## Cutler & Co — https://cutlerandco.com.au/
**Why chosen:** Editorial gold standard for a restaurant site. Treats the place as photojournalism subject, not a venue to promote. Restraint on copy, oversized imagery, magazine-grade typography hierarchy.

**Execution takeaways for Culantro:**
- Centered nav, bordered "specialty" content blocks (see the CUTLER CLASSICS / CHEESE SELECTION boxes on the menu page) — Culantro can use the same pattern for "Signatures" / "From the Rotisserie."
- Menu presented as one long vertical list with dotted-leader pricing, not cards — fits Peruvian menu density better than gridded product cards.
- Generous whitespace on either side of a narrow central column (max-width ~720px for text blocks).
- Serif display type for headings carries more gravitas than sans-serif on a restaurant site.

**Do NOT copy:** Cutler's stark monochrome palette. Culantro's identity is warm, mural-rich, celebratory — pure black-on-cream would erase the Peruvian character. Steal the *structure*, not the *mood*.

## Mission Ceviche — https://www.missionceviche.com/
**Why chosen:** Same cuisine (modern Peruvian). Annabel specifically called out the two-location picker as the IA pattern Culantro should use. Dark navy + gold accent proves the editorial pattern works with color.

**Execution takeaways for Culantro:**
- Two-location cards high on the homepage — photo + address + hours + dual CTAs ("Menu" / "Reserve") — this is the exact pattern Culantro needs to replace their current "Ferndale | Ann Arbor" text buttons over a single flame image.
- Dark header/footer with a bright accent color (mustard/gold in their case) — validates keeping Culantro's existing dark-background instinct while modernizing the execution.
- Food photo grid in a 3-up or 4-up row with no captions — lets dishes speak visually. Culantro has strong photos on Yelp/IG we can pull.

**Do NOT copy:** Their navy-and-mustard palette. Culantro should lean terracotta / rust / ochre — Peruvian flame, not coastal navy.

## Gjelina — https://www.gjelina.com/
**Why chosen:** Multi-location hub pattern done well. Warm peach/salmon background with red/coral accent proves "editorial + colorful" works at restaurant scale. Mid-point between Cutler's austerity and Tacombi's casual playfulness.

**Execution takeaways for Culantro:**
- Paired location cards (Venice | NY | Las Vegas | Take Away | Hotel) in a 2-up grid — each card has a location photo, bold brand mark, address block. Exactly the IA Culantro needs for its two spots.
- Warm cream/peach background instead of pure white — softens the page, lets photos pop without needing borders or drop-shadows.
- Small red accent underlines + dividers carry the brand color through without overwhelming.
- Serif logotype repeated per card — Culantro's ornate logo is strong enough to repeat in each location card header.

**Do NOT copy:** Gjelina's 5-location scope. Culantro is two spots + owner-operator — copy weight should lean personal/hand-crafted, not "hospitality group."

## Synthesis for Culantro's mockup

**The formula:** Cutler's restraint × Gjelina's warm color × Mission Ceviche's two-location IA, all filtered through Culantro's existing warm-gold-on-black brand.

**Palette direction (proposed, not yet validated):**
- Background: **warm cream / bone** (#F5EEE3 or similar) OR **deep rust** (#2A1410) — test both in mockup
- Primary text: charcoal #1A1410 (on cream) or cream #F5EEE3 (on rust)
- Accent: flame orange **#E8661F** (warmer than #FF8924) for CTAs + dividers
- Gold: preserve **#C59D5F** for section marks and logo
- Secondary accent: deep terracotta **#B14B2D** for underlines (Gjelina-style)

**Typography direction:**
- Display heading: a warm serif (Playfair Display, Cormorant Garamond, or EB Garamond) — replaces current Alegreya small-caps
- Body: a humanist sans (Inter or a lighter alternative — per memory `feedback_mockup_aesthetics.md`, lean away from Montserrat)
- Keep a script-display callout font *only* for the Culantro logo/wordmark, never for body copy

**Layout direction:**
- Hero: single oversized food photo (pollo a la brasa flame shot or ceviche plating), editorial caption, no carousel
- Location picker: two side-by-side cards (Mission Ceviche pattern) with location photo + hours + address + Menu/Reserve CTAs
- Signatures block: Cutler-style bordered "From the Rotisserie" list with dotted-leader pricing
- Mural/process strip: horizontal scroll of hand-painted murals + build-out shots — turns Culantro's current gallery noise into a feature (hand-crafted story)
- Reviews: 2-3 pull quotes with source badges (Yelp Elite, TripAdvisor)
- Footer: social + addresses per location, no phone CTA (unverified)
