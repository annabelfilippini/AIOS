# Pepper Pong — Video Walkthrough Script

**Target:** 60–90 sec. Conversational, not read-aloud.
**Tabs open before recording:**
1. `pepperpong.com` (homepage, scrolled to top)
2. `pepper-pong-audit.vercel.app` (landing page ready)

Optional: a second browser profile or incognito so no logged-in admin UI shows.

---

## 0:00 – 0:08 — Open

> "Hey Tom, I'm Annabel — made a quick walk-through of Pepper Pong. Three things I found, then I'll show you what a fix could look like."

*[Start on pepperpong.com homepage.]*

---

## 0:08 – 0:40 — Three findings on the live site

**Finding 1 — Schools isn't in the nav** *(scroll to top, highlight nav)*

> "Your Schools page is actually the best-structured page on the whole site — it sells a $599 product to educators. But it's not in your main nav. A PE teacher Googling 'PE games for schools' would never find their way to it."

**Finding 2 — Vocabulary mismatch** *(briefly scroll the homepage copy)*

> "When people search 'mini pickleball set' or 'tabletop paddle game' — which is how a normal person would describe this — you're invisible on Google. Your site calls it a 'portable paddle sport.' Nobody types that. This isn't a redesign problem, it's a content one."

**Finding 3 — No announcement bar** *(pause at the top of the page)*

> "You've got an active NIRSA deal, bundle discounts, free shipping — but there's nothing at the top of the page surfacing any of it. Visitors are missing your best offers."

---

## 0:40 – 1:15 — Redesign reveal

*[Switch tabs to `pepper-pong-audit.vercel.app`, click into the homepage mockup.]*

> "Here's a quick mockup of what this could look like."

*[Point out, in order:]*

1. **Announcement bar at the top** — rotating through current promos.
2. **Schools added to the nav** — one click from the homepage to the $599 product.
3. **Homepage copy uses 'mini pickleball' and 'tabletop game'** alongside the Pepper Pong brand voice — so Google can actually connect searchers to the site.

> "The brand feel is still yours — same coral, same personality, same hero testimonials. I just made the things people already search for easier to find."

---

## 1:15 – 1:30 — Close

*[Back on the portal landing page briefly.]*

> "I pulled this together as a full audit plus a growth dashboard too — happy to share if it's useful. Either way, hope this is helpful."

---

## Do / Don't

- **Do** record in one take if it feels natural. Re-record whole; don't edit.
- **Do** keep the mouse slow and deliberate — fast cursor movement reads frantic.
- **Don't** read verbatim. Use the script as guardrails, not a teleprompter.
- **Don't** apologize or hedge ("sorry if this is...", "I'm no expert but...") — the work is the credibility.
- **Don't** go past 1:30 — if you hit 2:00 on the timer, re-record shorter.
