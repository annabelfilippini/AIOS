## Key Findings

### What's working
1. **Branded search is locked down.** Anyone searching "pepper pong" finds them immediately.
2. **"Portable ping pong" is a win.** #1 organic for a solid category term.
3. **Long-tail pickleball+ping pong combos work.** When searchers use both sport names, Pepper Pong surfaces.

### What's broken
1. **Category invisibility.** Pepper Pong doesn't rank for the generic terms people use when they don't know the product exists yet ("tabletop paddle game," "mini pickleball game"). This is where discovery happens.
2. **Missing from every "best of" list.** No presence on family game roundups, gift guides, or Shark Tank "best products" compilations. These drive discovery traffic.
3. **School market is dark.** Has products and pricing for education but zero search visibility. The Schools page has no meta description, isn't in the nav, and uses none of the terms PE teachers search for.
4. **No content for non-branded discovery.** The site has product pages and a story page, but no blog, no guides, no "best tabletop games for..." content. Every competitor ranking above them on category terms has content marketing.
5. **Copy doesn't match search language.** Pepper Pong calls itself a "portable paddle sport." Searchers look for "tabletop game," "mini pickleball," "indoor ping pong." The vocabulary mismatch means search engines don't connect the product to the queries.

### Top 3 SEO recommendations for audit
1. **Add "tabletop pickleball" and "mini ping pong" language to product pages and homepage.** The site never uses the terms people actually search. This is the single highest-impact fix.
2. **Optimize the Schools page** — add meta description, add to main nav, target "PE games" and "indoor school sports equipment" keywords. There's a $599 product with zero discoverability.
3. **Start a blog or resource section** targeting "best tabletop games for families," "office break room games," "indoor games for any surface." Create the content that "best of" lists link to.

## Tool Audit: What Works for SEO Research

Tested during this walk-through. Critical for building the `/audit-seo` skill.

### Works (agent-automated)
| Tool | What it gives | How to access |
|------|--------------|---------------|
| **WebSearch** | SERP position (who ranks where for any keyword) | Direct tool call |
| **Google Autocomplete API** | Real search behavior, volume proxy (4 vs 10 suggestions) | `suggestqueries.google.com/complete/search?client=firefox&q=KEYWORD` via WebFetch |
| **pytrends (Google Trends)** | Interest over time, top states/countries. Partial — 429 after ~2 calls | Python library, `pip install pytrends`. Prioritize interest_over_time + interest_by_region. Skip related_queries (always 429s). |
| **DIY AnswerThePublic** | Question/comparison/audience queries people actually search | Autocomplete API with prefixes: "how to", "what is", "is", "vs", "for [segment]" |

### Blocked (requires manual browser or paid subscription)
| Tool | Why it fails | When to use |
|------|-------------|-------------|
| **Google Keyword Planner** | Requires Google Ads OAuth | For exact monthly volume — free with Google Ads account |
| **Ubersuggest** | Login wall, JS-rendered | Keyword difficulty scores, free tier limited |
| **Google Trends (full)** | pytrends rate-limited after 2 calls | Annabel opens browser for deeper geographic/comparison data |

### What we don't need
- **Ahrefs/SEMrush** ($99-139/mo) — the automated stack above gives 90% of the insight for $0. Reconsider only if doing 10+ audits/month and clients expect exact volume numbers.

## Enhanced Findings (post-autocomplete)

### Vocabulary mismatch is the #1 problem
People search "mini pickleball set" (10 autocomplete suggestions, active category). Pepper Pong calls itself a "portable paddle sport." The bridge term is there — "portable ping pong" already works and even includes a Shark Tank autocomplete (#10). But the mini pickleball category, which has MORE search activity, is completely missed.

### The corporate market is real and untapped
10 autocomplete suggestions for "indoor game for office" with specific variants (employees, teams, Fun Friday, Christmas party). This is a buyer persona Pepper Pong's site doesn't acknowledge at all. A single landing page — "Pepper Pong for the Office" — targeting these terms could open an entirely new revenue channel.

### The education market searches internationally
"PE equipment for schools" autocomplete includes UK, Ireland, Australia, NZ. If Pepper Pong's school play is US-only, there's latent international demand. Even for the US market, the Schools page needs a complete SEO overhaul — meta description, main nav placement, and content targeting "PE equipment," "indoor PE games," and "school sports equipment."

### Discovery is the real problem, not conversion
Branded search is strong (10 autocomplete suggestions, dominant SERP position). Anyone who knows "Pepper Pong" can find and buy it. The gap is in **discovery** — the people who would love this product but don't know it exists yet. They're searching "mini pickleball game," "fun office game," "best tabletop games for adults," and Pepper Pong isn't there.
