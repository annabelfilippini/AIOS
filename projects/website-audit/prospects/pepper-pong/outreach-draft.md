## Structure rules (distilled for skill)

1. **Greeting on first name.** No "Dear," no title.
2. **One sentence on the video.** Name the channel (Drive), name the deliverable (walk-through), gesture at the substance (number of findings + mockup). Don't summarize findings — the video does that.
3. **One sentence on the portal.** Soft offer, not a pitch. "If anything sparks" > "I'd love to hop on a call."
4. **One sentence close.** Warm, no ask. "Hope it's useful" > "Let me know what you think."
5. **No money. No "complimentary." No "portfolio."** The video already showed the work — value is already established. Don't underprice or self-justify.
6. **Sign-off:** first name only. No title, no signature block, no links.

## What to personalize per prospect

- **[First name]** — pulled from sheet `contact_name` column.
- **[Site domain]** — pulled from sheet `url` column, strip protocol.
- **Number of findings** — match the video script (usually 3).
- **Portal URL** — `https://<prospect-slug>-audit.vercel.app`.
- **Subject line** — pick the variant that sounds natural for this contact (warmer for warm leads, more neutral for semi-cold).

## Send checklist

- [ ] Drive share sent first (or scheduled within minutes). The email is the follow-up, not the first touch.
- [ ] Portal URL loads and looks right on mobile (prospect likely opens on phone).
- [ ] First name is correct spelling.
- [ ] No typos in the domain.
- [ ] Reads out loud in under 10 seconds.

## Notes

- **Why so short?** The video did 80% of the selling. The email is context + next step. A long email undercuts the video by implying it wasn't enough.
- **Why mention the portal here and not in the video?** Video close already says "happy to share the audit and dashboard if useful" — this email makes that offer concrete (clickable link). Video = pull, email = push-to-action.
- **Not sending for Tom.** He's family, Drive share is enough, the hypothetical exists for skill extraction only.
