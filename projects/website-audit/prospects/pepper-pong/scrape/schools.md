# The PE Game That Builds Social-Emotional Skills While Every Kid Plays

60-second setup. Every student engaged. Supports physical literacy and SEL standards. Volume pricing for schools and districts.

**Pepper Pong is a simple, portable, racket-and-ball game** that encourages students to be active, interact with peers, and enjoy play in a positive, inclusive environment.

In physical education settings, games like Pepper Pong naturally promote **communication, cooperation, and respect for others** — key aspects of social-emotional learning.

Research on games in PE shows that play activities contribute to social skills such as sharing, cooperation, rule negotiation, and conflict resolution, while also supporting **self-confidence and emotional regulation**.

Because Pepper Pong brings students together — laughing, cheering, and moving — it supports not just physical fitness, but **positive peer relationships and emotional well-being**.
