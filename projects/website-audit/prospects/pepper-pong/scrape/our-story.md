## PEPPER PONG IS A TESTAMENT TO TRANSFORMING LIFE'S DARKEST MOMENTS INTO BRILLIANT JOY.

For years, my dream to create Pepper Pong was held captive by alcohol addiction. It was only through becoming sober in 2016 that I found the clarity, connection and courage to bring it to life.

This game is a tribute to those who helped me achieve and maintain sobriety, including all who get in on the action - which I hope will include you!

I hope it brings you joy, fosters friendships and perhaps serves as a reminder that any struggle can become a triumph and that authentic human connection - facilitated by a game like ours - is a key ingredient.
