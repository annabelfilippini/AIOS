A Southern Italian Twist on a Korean Classic

Cacio e Pepe Tteokbokki

$18 · from the dinner menu

Chef Ji Hye Kim is obsessed with ancient Korean culinary texts and the finer points of fermentation — and this dish is where that obsession meets Rome. Chewy Korean rice cakes are tossed with pecorino, black pepper, and just enough butter to gloss each piece, then finished with pickled shallots and green scallion.

As in Korea, one of the most popular dishes at Miss Kim has been the spicy, pork-scented, gochujang-laced Street Style Tteokbokki. The Cacio e Pepe is the cross-cultural counterpart — the same rice cake, reimagined through a Southern Italian lens.

Featured on the Zingerman's Community blog, January 2025

How to get here

Our street address is on Fifth Avenue but our entrance is closer to Kingsley and directly off of the one way parking lot located at Kingsley (entrance) and Fourth Ave (exit). We suggest that you park in this small lot when you come to pick up your food.

If you've already parked on Fifth, you can still find us by walking all the way through the courtyard between Sweetwaters and Found to the small parking lot on the other side.
