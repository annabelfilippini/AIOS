Our Philosophy

Hospitality, three ways.

When Y.B. Ko and Duc Tang set out to create Pacific Rim, the driving idea was hospitality — and how a restaurant could extend it genuinely to everyone who walked in.

I.

Relationship

We believe cultivating good relationships is the cornerstone of our restaurant — treating our staff with dignity, and our guests with the warmth of being invited over for dinner.

II.

Honesty

From procuring ingredients in the field to plating the final dish, we search for what is natural, fresh and wholesome — and we prepare it from scratch, without shortcuts.

III.

Service

Service that is warm, friendly and attentive — for our guests, for our staff, and for the community of Ann Arbor that has made this restaurant possible.

The Chef

Duc Tang

Born in Saigon and raised in California, Duc arrived in Ann Arbor with a biochemistry degree from Yale and a graduate degree in theology — and no formal culinary training. In 2000 he partnered with graduate-school friend Y.B. Ko to re-imagine Mrs. Ko’s Korean kitchen as a pan-Asian dining room.

Today, as sole proprietor, Duc continues to cook the food he grew up helping his mother make — informed by French technique, rooted in seasonality, and unmistakably his own.

“I am as surprised as anybody that I ended up being a chef.”
