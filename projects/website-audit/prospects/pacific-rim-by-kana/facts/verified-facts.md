## History (from About page)
- 1982: Opened as **Kana** — small family-owned Korean restaurant near UM Hospital by Mr. and Mrs. Ko
- 1995: Moved to current downtown Ann Arbor location
- 2000: Son **Y.B. Ko** took over and partnered with grad-school friend **Duc Tang** to expand menu; renamed **Pacific Rim by Kana**
- 2007: Y.B. Ko moved to Hawaii; Duc Tang became sole proprietor

## Chef
**Duc Tang** — born Saigon, Vietnam. Family fled at age 8, through refugee camps in Hong Kong and Philippines to California in 1980. Yale (Biochemistry), graduate degree in Theology and Biblical Studies at Regent College in Vancouver. No formal culinary training. Quote: "I am as surprised as anybody that I ended up being a chef."

## Philosophy (About page — Y.B.'s framing)
Hospitality is centered on three ideas:
1. **Relationship** — "treating our staff with dignity and respect" / "treating our guests with a sense of warmth and welcome"
2. **Honesty** — ingredients that are "natural, fresh and wholesome" / prepared "authentic and healthy — making things from scratch"
3. **Service** — "warm, friendly and attentive" service that extends to staff and community
