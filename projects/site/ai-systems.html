AI Systems

AI should give people time back for the work only they can do.

I build small, useful systems around the work that drains time: writing, researching, organizing, following up, and turning one good idea into many usable pieces.

Four useful starting points

  • Content assistant: turn launches, events, photos, and notes into newsletter and social drafts.
  • Outreach system: research and draft sponsor, partner, creator, or customer follow-up.
  • Customer helper: organize common questions and draft consistent responses.
  • Internal AI workspace: reusable prompts and examples in the voice of the business.

What I care about

The goal is not to automate personality out of the business. The goal is to clear repetitive work so owners can spend more time on taste, relationships, judgment, community, and momentum.

I like systems that are small enough to actually use, clear enough to explain, and flexible enough to improve as the business learns.
