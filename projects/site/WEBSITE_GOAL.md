# AnnabelFilippini.com Goal

Last updated: 2026-05-13

## North Star

AnnabelFilippini.com should become the home base for all Annabel Filippini-related credibility, work, writing, experiments, and ways to collaborate.

The site should feel like a more complete, more human LinkedIn profile: a place someone can visit when they want to understand who Annabel is, what she is building, what she knows how to do, why she is credible, and whether they should work with her, hire her, refer her, interview her, or follow what she is making.

The site should answer one question quickly:

**Why should I trust Annabel with work that sits between AI, systems, small business, and human judgment?**

## Strategic Role

The site is not only a portfolio. It is not only a consulting landing page. It is not only a personal website.

It is a credibility hub with clear doors for different audiences:

- Consultants who may want to partner with Annabel or refer clients.
- Companies and small businesses considering working with Annabel.
- AI builders, operators, and technically curious people who want to see what she is experimenting with.
- Recruiters, collaborators, podcast hosts, mentors, and professional contacts who want the fuller picture.

The site should make Annabel feel:

- credible without sounding inflated
- curious without feeling scattered
- technical without becoming cold
- human without losing professional seriousness
- early-career without feeling inexperienced
- experimental without feeling unproven

## Core Positioning

Annabel helps people and businesses figure out where AI belongs, what should not be automated yet, and how to turn messy work into clearer systems.

The strongest positioning is not "I build AI tools." It is:

**I help people use AI to create more time, stronger systems, and more room for the human work that actually matters.**

This should show up across the site through:

- AI Operating System thinking
- audit-led diagnosis before implementation
- small business and consultant-friendly language
- proof from real projects
- a visible lab of experiments and learning
- a skills page that shows tools, judgment, and process

## What The Agency Audit Network Adds

The Agency Audit Network project gives the website a sharper professional wedge.

The important idea: Annabel can become a trusted diagnostic and routing layer for businesses that know AI matters but do not know what to ask for, what to build first, or which agency/operator to trust.

That means the site should eventually make room for:

- a clear AIOS Starting Point Audit offer
- a Quick Read or mini-audit entry point
- examples of audit outputs and business diagnoses
- a partner/referral frame for consultants and agencies
- proof that Annabel understands acquisition, delivery, support, and operations
- language around trust, clarity, and routing instead of hype

The site should avoid promising implementation outcomes unless a project scope or partner is confirmed. It should promise clarity, diagnosis, prioritization, and good next steps.

## Audience Paths

### 1. Companies and Small Businesses

They need to feel:

- Annabel understands business owners and operators.
- She can make AI practical without overcomplicating it.
- She will not push automation where it does not belong.
- She can diagnose what is worth doing first.
- There is a clear next step if they want help.

Primary CTA:

**Find my AI starting point** or **Request an AIOS Quick Read**

Content they need:

- simple explanation of AIOS
- audit offer
- examples of business problems Annabel helps with
- proof from Cooldown, Shopify, website audits, and small business work
- contact path

### 2. Consultants and Agencies

They need to feel:

- Annabel is a high-trust front door, not a random lead broker.
- She understands how to diagnose and scope work before referring it.
- She can produce useful briefs that make implementation easier.
- She cares about client fit, partner fit, and not overpromising.

Primary CTA:

**Partner with me** or **Talk about client fit**

Content they need:

- Agency Audit Network explanation
- partner criteria
- examples of audit/matching logic
- clear values around disclosure, trust, and scope
- contact path

### 3. AI Geeks, Builders, and Operators

They need to feel:

- Annabel is actually building and experimenting, not just using AI as a buzzword.
- She understands agents, workflows, reusable skills, CLI tools, and compound learning loops.
- Her technical curiosity is paired with taste and human judgment.

Primary CTA:

**Explore the lab** or **See my skills**

Content they need:

- Lab page with live experiments and build notes
- Skills page with tools, workflows, and examples
- AI-OS explanation in plain English
- links to projects, artifacts, demos, screenshots, or notes

### 4. General Professional Credibility

They need to feel:

- Annabel is a real person with a clear professional identity.
- The Michigan, Okta, AI, small business, and builder threads connect.
- Her interests are wide but not random.

Primary CTA:

**Start here** or **Contact Annabel**

Content they need:

- short biography
- credibility strip
- featured projects
- media/podcast quotes
- current work and future direction

## Recommended Site Navigation

The site should eventually use a simple navigation like:

1. Home
2. Work With Me
3. Skills
4. Lab
5. About
6. Contact

Optional later additions:

- Writing
- Proof
- Media
- Partners

Do not add too many top-level tabs too soon. The homepage can route people to audience-specific paths while the navigation stays simple.

## Homepage Flow

Inspired by Jenna Kutcher's site structure, but translated into Annabel's world:

1. **Hero: who Annabel is and why she matters**
   - Name should be prominent.
   - One clear positioning line.
   - Primary CTA for businesses.
   - Secondary CTA for skills/lab.

2. **Credibility snapshot**
   - University of Michigan School of Information
   - Incoming Okta Product Analyst
   - AI-OS builder
   - Small business / Shopify / audit work
   - Podcast or media mention when useful

3. **Audience doors**
   - For businesses: AIOS Starting Point / audit
   - For consultants: partner/referral network
   - For AI builders: lab and technical experiments
   - For general professional context: about/story

4. **Core belief**
   - AI should create more room for human judgment, not strip the soul out of the work.
   - This section should carry the emotional center of the site.

5. **Featured work**
   - Cooldown / Shopify redesign
   - Website audit examples
   - Agency Audit Network / AIOS audit framework
   - AI-OS / agent workflow experiments
   - Wayloft, Spent, or other lab projects

6. **Skills and tools**
   - A preview of the Skills page.
   - Show categories, not just tool names.
   - Emphasize judgment: research, diagnosis, workflows, AI systems, UX, data storytelling.

7. **Lab preview**
   - Current experiments.
   - What Annabel is learning.
   - What feedback she wants.

8. **Personal credibility and story**
   - Short, warm about section.
   - The thread: Michigan -> product/data/UX -> Okta -> AI + small business systems.

9. **Contact**
   - Clear routes:
     - I want an audit.
     - I want to partner.
     - I want to talk about AI/workflows.
     - I just want to connect.

## Proof To Collect

To make the site stronger over time, collect:

- screenshots of audits and reports
- before/after website screenshots
- testimonials from Cooldown and other small businesses
- podcast quotes and media mentions
- lab screenshots and demo links
- short case studies
- partner quotes
- project metrics where available

## Tone And Design Direction

The site should feel editorial, warm, credible, and a little distinctive.

Keep:

- big Annabel Filippini first-viewport identity
- warm color palette from the current site
- strong typography
- friendly but professional copy
- visual proof through screenshots, project cards, and real artifacts

Avoid:

- generic AI consultant language
- overstuffed menus
- vague "future of work" claims with no proof
- too many unrelated experiments on the homepage
- making the site feel like only a resume
- making the site feel like only a sales funnel

## Current Website Direction To Keep

The current site already has useful pieces:

- strong visual identity with the large Annabel Filippini hero
- a warm, memorable palette
- audience doorways
- Skills and Lab pages
- practical AI and small business positioning

The next improvement is strategy, not more redesign churn:

- clarify the homepage flow
- keep Skills as the clearer public language
- add a Work With Me / AIOS Audit page
- make Lab more proof-driven
- add stronger credibility and case-study sections
- make Contact route different audiences

## Decision Rule

When deciding whether something belongs on the site, ask:

**Does this help a consultant, company, AI builder, or professional contact understand why Annabel is credible and what door they should walk through next?**

If yes, add it.

If it is interesting but does not build credibility, explain Annabel's direction, or create a useful next step, save it for the Lab or leave it out.
