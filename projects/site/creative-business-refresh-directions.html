A note from Annabel

I help small business owners get their time, focus, and momentum back.

Some of the best small businesses are run by people who are wildly creative, deeply thoughtful, and incredibly good at what they do.

But too often, the work that keeps the business alive starts crowding out the work that made the business special in the first place: the admin, website updates, notes, follow-ups, internal systems, outreach, finances, and tiny tasks that quietly eat the day.

I help passionate entrepreneurs use AI, automation, and better digital systems to make their business feel lighter, sharper, and more ready for its next chapter.

The goal is not to make your business feel more robotic. The goal is to make it feel more like you.

My job is to understand what you are trying to build, where your energy is being drained, and what would make the biggest difference first. Then I help make that thing real.

Who I am
Michigan grad, Denver-raised, deeply curious about AI.

I recently graduated from the University of Michigan, and I have spent the past year learning how tools like Claude, Codex, automation builders, coworking agents, and workflow systems can work together in ways that are genuinely useful for small businesses.

What this can look like
  • Cooldown A cleaner, stronger website for a small business leader who had been thinking about the refresh for months and needed help making it happen.
  • Marta Notes and automation for a life coach who wanted to be more present in sessions and spend less time buried in follow-up work.
  • Charter business Practical AI onboarding and operations support for a team learning how these tools could fit into their everyday work.
Ways I can help

Website refreshes, notes, follow-ups, outreach, finances, internal systems, team training, founder reminders, and small workflows that make the business feel more sound and spacious.

Use this when Cooldown wants to introduce you warmly. It feels like a beautiful attachment someone can forward with a personal note, not a generic menu of services.

My recommendation

Lead with the founder letter, support it with the website.

The founder letter has the most "you" in it. The website gives people somewhere to land once they are curious. The referral note makes it easy for Cooldown to actually send.

Make first

A two-page Founder Letter PDF with the case stories and your bio woven in.

Then build

A matching one-page website with the same visual language and shorter copy.

Give Cooldown

The PDF, the link, and the exact referral blurb so they can send it easily.

Avoid

Anything that looks like a generic AI agency, audit checklist, or student portfolio.
