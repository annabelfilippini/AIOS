Website Audit

Turn your website into a clearer, easier-to-run front door.

I look for the places where your site can be clearer for customers and easier for you to run.

Best for Shopify shops, local service businesses, creator-led brands, wellness/retail/community businesses, and owners who know the site could be working harder.

What I look for

  • Homepage messaging and customer journey
  • Mobile experience and navigation
  • Product, service, booking, or inquiry pages
  • Calls to action and conversion moments
  • Shopify sections, metafields, and owner editability
  • SEO basics, alt text, page structure, and accessibility

What you can get

Audit notes A prioritized list of what matters most and why.
Redesign help Implementation on the pages or templates that matter most.
Handoff guide A simple guide so the team can keep the site current.
